Sector: Genomic medicine
Location: Cambridge, MA
Summary: In vivo CRISPR with a Phase 3 win; heading into commercial transition.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Co-founded 2014 with Caribou Biosciences and Atlas Venture, licensing foundational CRISPR-Cas9 technology from UC Berkeley. Scientific co-founders include Jennifer Doudna, who shared the 2020 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for CRISPR-Cas9, along with Derrick Rossi, Rodolphe Barrangou, Erik Sontheimer, Luciano Marraffini, Andy May, Nessan Bermingham and Rachel Haurwitz.

PLATFORM

In vivo CRISPR-Cas9 genome editing delivered by liver-targeting lipid nanoparticles. The LNP delivery system is the linchpin — achieving durable, precise edits in a relevant tissue in adult patients has been the field's central problem.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Lonvo-z (NTLA-2002) — hereditary angioedema. Phase 3 HAELO hit: a single infusion reduced attacks 87% versus placebo over the six-month efficacy period; mean monthly attack rate 0.26 versus 2.10. FDA acceptance of the BLA expected H2 2026, PDUFA 10 March 2027, US launch H1 2027.
- Nex-z — transthyretin amyloidosis, advancing.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Public, NASDAQ NTLA. Approximately $628M in cash and marketable securities at the end of Q2 2026, expected to fund operations at least into 2028. Q1 2026 collaboration revenue $15.0M and net loss $96.2M; an April equity raise added about $207M.
