Sector: Genomic medicine
Location: Boston, MA (now a Lilly site)
Summary: Acquired by Lilly in July 2025; its base-editing programme continues inside Lilly.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded to apply base editing to cardiovascular disease, with the thesis that a single treatment could permanently lower cholesterol instead of requiring lifelong statins. The company no longer exists independently. This entry is retrospective and is kept for three reasons: the site is still in Boston, the programme is still running, and the acquisition is the clearest example in this file of how Boston genomic-medicine assets are now exiting.

PLATFORM

In vivo base editing delivered to the liver. Base editing changes a single DNA letter without cutting the double strand, which is a different risk profile from nuclease editing. The technology traces to Beam Therapeutics, which also has an entry in this file, and Lilly bought Beam's opt-in rights to this programme as part of assembling the deal.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- VERVE-102 — a potential first-in-class in vivo base editor targeting PCSK9, for atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease. It holds FDA Fast Track designation.
- The programme continues under Lilly and is one reason Lilly's genetic medicine site matters in this file.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Acquired by Eli Lilly. Completion was announced 25 July 2025 — note the date, because this file previously implied a 2026 transaction. Terms were $10.50 per share, roughly $1B, plus one non-tradeable contingent value right per share worth up to a further $3. The CVR pays only if the first patient is dosed with VERVE-102 in a US Phase 3 trial in atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease within ten years of closing. Lilly already held opt-in rights from a 2023 agreement, and had bought Beam's opt-in rights covering VERVE-102.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Hiring now runs through Lilly, not Verve. See the Lilly Seaport Innovation Center entry: the acquired team is part of why Lilly is the most acquisitive buyer in this market, and absorbing technical teams is usually the point of these deals.

ASSESSMENT

Do not apply here; apply to Lilly. The useful content is the structure of the exit. A ten-year CVR tied to Phase 3 dosing tells you Lilly priced most of the value as contingent, which is a sober view of in vivo base editing. For this file the entry is a pointer: the people are still in Boston, the programme is alive, and the employer's name has changed.

KEY FACTS

- Acquired by Eli Lilly; completion announced 25 July 2025
- Terms: $10.50 per share plus a CVR worth up to $3
- Lead programme VERVE-102, an in vivo base editor targeting PCSK9
- The CVR pays only on first Phase 3 dosing within ten years
