Summary: Bought by Sanofi for $9.1B, and now being dismantled in Cambridge.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

A Cambridge precision-medicine company built on kinase inhibitors for genomically defined diseases. It no longer operates independently. This entry is retrospective, and it is the most negative outcome in this group for anyone looking for a Cambridge seat, because the acquisition is being followed by closure rather than expansion.

PLATFORM

Targeted kinase inhibition against defined genomic drivers. Its science is now part of Sanofi's portfolio.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Assets transferred to Sanofi on closing.
- Percy Carter, Blueprint's Chief Scientific Officer, sits on Cellarity's board — one of several cross-links between entries in this file.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Acquired by Sanofi. The deal closed in July 2025 at $9.1B plus $400M in earnouts.

TEAMS AND ROLES

This is the part that matters. Sanofi is cutting 229 legacy Blueprint staff between October 2026 and June 2027 and closing two Cambridge offices. Senior leadership has largely departed. The market section of this file logs the same event among the 2026 contractions. There is no hiring here.

ASSESSMENT

A $9.1B acquisition that produced 229 redundancies and two office closures in Cambridge inside eighteen months. Keep the entry as evidence for the market read in this file: a large acquisition price is not a signal of local employment, and in this market it often predicts the opposite. The practical use is negative screening — do not treat Sanofi's Cambridge presence as a growing one — and as a source of displaced senior people worth knowing, since 229 experienced Cambridge staff will be looking for work through to mid-2027.
