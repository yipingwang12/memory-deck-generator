Sector: Adjacent - plant genomics
Location: Cambridge, MA
Summary: 07 rated it healthy and best-fit. It cut a third of its workforce in January 2026.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Flagship Pioneering company. Branded as "the SEEDesign company". Ponsi Trivisvavet is the CEO and a director. She frames the mission around three goals: food security, sustainability, and farmer profitability. She also argues that the addressable market is wider than seed, because a better seed changes how much water and fertiliser a farm needs.

PLATFORM

SEEDesign — seed development combining AI-enabled predictive design with a multiplex gene editing toolbox, applied to corn, soy and wheat. The CEO calls the machine-learning half "predictive design". The two halves are meant to work as one loop: the models choose the edits, and the editing toolbox makes them at scale.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Crop seed products rather than a clinical pipeline.
- Corn, soy and wheat are the named crops.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

$771M raised; a $2.17B valuation attached to the January 2025 financing. An earlier Series C raised $89M. Named to TIME's America's Top GreenTech Companies 2026. Appointed Ryan Rapp to the board effective 11 May 2026.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Inari cut about 33% of its workforce in January 2026 and gave "changing business needs" as the reason. The company filed a WARN notice with the Indiana Department of Workforce Development on 20 January 2026 for 64 employees at West Lafayette. Separations ran to 6 February 2026. The letter states the cuts are permanent and that there are no bumping rights. Chief People Officer Mark Berry signed it. Massachusetts staff were also affected, but no Massachusetts WARN notice was publicly filed. Note the arithmetic: the 33% figure is company-wide, and the 64 figure is the Indiana site only. Inari did not disclose its total headcount, so the two numbers cannot be reconciled from outside.

ASSESSMENT

07 rated Inari Healthy and the strongest adjacent fit by far. The valuation and the technology are intact but a third of the company is gone, so that rating no longer holds. Still worth a conversation — post-layoff companies that keep their platform often rebuild around a smaller technical core — but it is not the safe pick 07 described.
