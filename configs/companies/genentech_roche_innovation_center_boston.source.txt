Location: 1 Milestone Street, Enterprise Research Campus, Allston
Summary: Just opened, tripled its lease, hiring up to 500. The newest large seat in Boston.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Roche announced the launch of the Roche Genentech Innovation Center Boston in March 2025, based at Harvard's Enterprise Research Campus in Allston, developed by Tishman Speyer. The grand opening was 17 September 2026 — four days before this dossier was written. Thomas Schinecker, CEO of the Roche Group, spoke at the opening. Governor Maura Healey and Mayor Michelle Wu also spoke. A state and city turnout at an opening is a reliable sign of a long commitment.

PLATFORM

This is Roche's global hub for cardiovascular, renal and metabolic disease, which the company shortens to CVRM. The site holds end-to-end R&D, not research alone. Roche also owns Foundation Medicine, so the research group and the diagnostics business sit under one corporate roof. Foundation Medicine has its own entry in this file.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Cardiovascular, renal and metabolism research, with clinical development on the same site.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Corporate. The lease runs ten years. Roche's own release of 17 September 2026 gives the size as 95,000 sq ft. Press coverage in 2025 described a tripling of the committed space from 30,000 sq ft and reported about 100,000 sq ft. Use the 95,000 figure, because it is primary. Move-in is phased from mid-2026. The centre forms part of a stated $50B Roche and Genentech commitment to US manufacturing and R&D.

TEAMS AND ROLES

The site has capacity for up to 500 people. Roche names four disciplines: discovery research, clinical development, artificial intelligence and data science. Two of those four are dry-lab, which is the reason this entry rates fit 3 on a new site with no track record. A March 2025 report said Manu Chakravarthy, Roche's global head of CVRM product development, would oversee the centre. No site head title was confirmed at the opening, so treat that name as unverified.
