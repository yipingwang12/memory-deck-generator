Sector: Neurotech - vagus nerve stimulation (bioelectronic medicine)
Location: Valencia, CA
Summary: Implanted vagus nerve stimulator approved for rheumatoid arthritis.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2007, Valencia, California. One source says the company was built on Kevin Tracey's discovery of the inflammatory reflex: vagus nerve signals that damp immune inflammation.

PLATFORM

The SetPoint System: a small implant on the left vagus nerve in the neck that gives one minute of stimulation a day to activate anti-inflammatory pathways. Neuroimmune modulation: a nerve implant that treats an immune disease.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- FDA approval (PMA P240039): 30 July 2025, adults with moderately to severely active rheumatoid arthritis and inadequate response to prior therapy.
- Reported as the first electrical / neuroimmune therapy approved for RA.
- RESET-RA: sham-controlled, 242 patients. In a subgroup with one prior biologic or csDMARD, ACR20 44.2% vs 19%. Related serious adverse events 1.7%.
- Rollout: limited cities in 2025; wider US launch from early 2026.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

2025: $140M private financing, with new commercial leadership.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Not researched. Valencia, CA.

ASSESSMENT

Widens the deck beyond the brain: the same neuromodulation logic applied to a peripheral nerve and an immune disease. The approval is the field's proof that bioelectronic medicine can clear the FDA. California.
