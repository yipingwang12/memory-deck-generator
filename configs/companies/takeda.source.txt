Sector: Established biopharma
Location: Kendall Square, Cambridge (new global R&D site at 585 Kendall Street)
Summary: Largest life-sciences employer in the state; vacating old space for a new 600,000 sq ft R&D home.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

A Japanese pharmaceutical company whose global research and development leadership sits in Cambridge. It is the largest life-sciences employer in Massachusetts, which is why its decisions move the market numbers in this file more than any other single company.

PLATFORM

Broad pharmaceutical portfolio. For this file the notable structural fact is outsourcing: Takeda is Nabla Bio's largest partner, so it buys generative protein design rather than building it. That is the pattern to understand about every large employer in this group — the computational capability is increasingly purchased, and the jobs move to the vendor.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Broad portfolio across its therapeutic areas.
- Cell therapy research has been discontinued, including the October 2025 shutdown of its cell-therapy operation, roughly 130 Cambridge jobs.
- A Cambridge director role posted 30 June 2026 covers external innovation in AI, automation, data and digital technologies for drug discovery — that is, buying capability rather than building teams.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Public. Transformation costs exceeded $940M in the current financial year, targeting more than $1.25B of annual savings by 2028. The property moves need reading together. Takeda is building One Cambridge Campus, announced June 2022: a new site of about 600,000 sq ft of lab and office at 585 Kendall Street, developed by BioMed Realty on a 15-year lease, to be its global research and development home. Reporting in early 2026 said it was on track to open later in the year. In parallel it put 630,153 sq ft on the sublease market at 300 Massachusetts Avenue and 35 and 40 Landsdowne Street, and separately marketed about 125,000 sq ft at 75 Binney Street, which it described as true excess capacity. Some sources give the new address as 585 Third Street.

TEAMS AND ROLES

247 Cambridge roles cut from 1 July 2026, running to December 2027, plus 387 elsewhere in the US, and following roughly 1,500 cuts across 2024-25. The Boston Globe reported a further 250-person cut in March 2026. No computational posting beyond the AI-sourcing director role was found.

ASSESSMENT

This entry needs a correction to how the file reads it. The market section treats the sublease as pure contraction, and the 247 cuts are real. But the sublease is of buildings Takeda is leaving in order to move into a purpose-built 600,000 sq ft global R&D headquarters in Kendall on a 15-year lease. Those are two different things, and conflating them overstates the retreat. The accurate read is a company shrinking its headcount while concentrating what remains into one new Cambridge campus. Still not a target — the hiring signal is a role whose job is to buy AI capability from outside — but the state's largest life-sciences employer is committing to Kendall for fifteen years, and the file should say so.

KEY FACTS

- Largest life-sciences employer in Massachusetts
- Building One Cambridge Campus, ~600,000 sq ft, as its global R&D home
- Nabla Bio's largest partner
- Discontinued cell-therapy research
- Cut 247 Cambridge roles from 1 July 2026
