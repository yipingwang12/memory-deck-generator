Sector: Established biopharma
Location: Boston Seaport (50 Northern Ave, Fan Pier)
Summary: The best combination of stability, growth and genetics on the list.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 1989 by Joshua Boger and Kevin J. Kinsella. Boger was CEO from 1992 to 2009, arriving from Merck where he was Senior Director of Basic Chemistry. Vertex was one of the first biotech firms to pursue an explicit strategy of rational, structure-based drug design rather than combinatorial chemistry — a lineage that matters, because it is a company built on computation from the start.

PLATFORM

Structure-based rational drug design, now spanning small molecules, cell therapy and gene editing. Casgevy, developed with CRISPR Therapeutics, was the first approved CRISPR therapy.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Povetacicept — rolling BLA completed for US accelerated approval in IgA nephropathy following positive Phase 3 interim data. PDUFA 30 November. Phase 3 portion initiated in primary membranous nephropathy; Phase 2 proof-of-concept in generalised myasthenia gravis.
- Casgevy — CRISPR therapy for sickle cell disease and beta thalassaemia; $116M of 2025 sales across 64 patient infusions.
- Cystic fibrosis franchise remains the revenue base.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Public, NASDAQ VRTX. Q1 2026 revenue $2.99B (+8% YoY); Q2 2026 revenue $3.33B (+12% YoY). Full-year 2026 guidance raised to $13.1-13.2B.

6,570 employees worldwide as of March 2026. Renewed its Seaport lease. At Fan Pier, 35% of the facility is lab space across 11 lab floors.
