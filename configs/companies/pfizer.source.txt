Sector: Established biopharma
Location: Kendall Square, Cambridge (610 Main Street)
Summary: A consolidated Kendall campus of about 1,000 R&D staff on MIT-owned land.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Pfizer moved research into Cambridge and then consolidated it. It leased 610 Main Street from an MIT subsidiary and brought three other leased Cambridge sites into that single campus. The landlord relationship is worth noting: the site sits on MIT-affiliated property in Kendall Square, so Pfizer is embedded in the same block as the academic institutions in this file.

PLATFORM

Broad portfolio. The Cambridge research areas reported are rare disease, cardiovascular and metabolic disease, inflammation and immunology, neuroscience, and advanced biotherapeutic technologies. Pfizer also runs the Boston Center for Therapeutic Innovation, an academic-partnership vehicle, alongside its BioTherapeutics Research and Development group.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Broad portfolio across the therapeutic areas above.
- The Center for Therapeutic Innovation model partners directly with academic investigators, which is a route in that does not require being a Pfizer employee.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Public, NYSE PFE. Footprint figures conflict and should be treated as approximate. This file recorded 280,000 sq ft, while the lease reporting describes the full 500,000 sq ft of 610 Main Street. Both may be true if the file's figure covers only part of the building. Around 1,000 R&D professionals is the consistent headcount figure across sources.

TEAMS AND ROLES

About 1,000 R&D staff at the Cambridge campus. The relevant note from elsewhere in this file is in 01: a Pfizer AI and ML role at Pearl River was written for a method builder rather than a method user, which is the keystone of the profile this dossier serves. That posting was not in Cambridge, but it shows the company writes roles of that shape.

ASSESSMENT

Stable rather than growing, and that is worth something in this market. The two useful specifics are the Center for Therapeutic Innovation, which collaborates with academics and so offers a non-employee route, and the evidence from 01 that Pfizer hires method builders in AI and ML. Kendall Square also makes the commute question easy. Worth checking its Cambridge requisitions directly rather than relying on the aggregate picture. Note that the site-level sources found this pass are old, so confirm the current footprint before quoting it.

KEY FACTS

- Kendall Square campus at 610 Main Street, leased from an MIT subsidiary
- Consolidated three other Cambridge sites into one campus
- About 1,000 R&D professionals
- Runs the Boston Center for Therapeutic Innovation
