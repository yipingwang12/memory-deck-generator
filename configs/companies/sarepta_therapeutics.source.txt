Sector: Genomic medicine
Location: Cambridge, MA
Summary: Duchenne gene therapy met patient deaths, a boxed warning and a 36% cut.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

A Cambridge company built around Duchenne muscular dystrophy, first with exon-skipping oligonucleotides and then with gene therapy. For years it was the clearest Boston success in rare neuromuscular disease. 2025 reversed that.

PLATFORM

Exon-skipping oligonucleotides and AAV gene therapy for Duchenne, plus an siRNA pipeline the company has since prioritised.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Elevidys — the AAV gene therapy for Duchenne. Two non-ambulatory teenagers who received it died of acute liver failure.
- The FDA asked for a boxed warning covering acute liver injury and acute liver failure, which Sarepta added. The company said this resolved the material issues for the ambulatory label.
- Sarepta first refused an FDA request to pause shipments and said it would keep supplying ambulatory patients, then later complied with a halt on all shipments. The sequence matters for how the company handles regulators.
- The restructuring refocused the pipeline toward siRNA programmes.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Public, NASDAQ SRPT. The share price fell more than 80% across 2025 and the company has $1B of senior notes due in 2027. The restructuring was projected to deliver about $400M in annual cost savings, with one-time severance of $32-37M booked in Q3 2025.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Cut about 36% of its workforce, roughly 500 people, with the reduction to be completed by early 2026. No current posting was enumerated. Treat hiring as frozen in the gene therapy organisation and possible only on the siRNA side.

ASSESSMENT

The worst safety outcome in this file, and the most instructive. Two deaths, a boxed warning, a public standoff with the FDA, 500 jobs gone and $1B of debt due in 2027. Do not pursue. Its value here is as a risk calibration for every AAV gene therapy entry in the dossier, and as a reminder that a company's conduct under regulatory pressure is itself information. Note also the market read: this is one of the single largest contributions to the Cambridge job losses logged in this file's market section.

KEY FACTS

- Cambridge; built on Duchenne muscular dystrophy therapies
- Elevidys is its AAV gene therapy for Duchenne
- FDA required a boxed warning for acute liver injury and failure
- Cut about 36% of its workforce, roughly 500 roles
- Refocused the pipeline toward siRNA programmes
