HISTORY AND PEOPLE

A Palo Alto company built on circulating tumour DNA. Its three businesses are therapy selection in advanced cancer, minimal residual disease monitoring, and now population screening. This entry was `carried` with the Boston seat unverified; no Massachusetts facility was found this pass, so it is confirmed as remote-or-relocate.

PLATFORM

Cell-free DNA assays. The company holds the patent estate covering cell-free DNA testing that it is now asserting against Tempus AI, which makes its intellectual property a business in its own right.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Shield — colorectal cancer screening. Q2 2026 screening revenue was $52.9M against $14.8M a year earlier, on volume near 66,000 tests against roughly 16,000. That is more than tripling.
- Shield was added to the American Cancer Society's colorectal screening guidelines, and UnitedHealth Group agreed to cover it. One summary puts the resulting covered population at about 70 million lives.
- Reveal — minimal residual disease. Volume more than doubled in Q2 2026.
- Therapy selection in advanced cancer remains the established base.
- Litigating two patent cases against Tempus AI in Delaware over cell-free DNA patents; its dismissal bids against Guardant's claims were denied in August 2026.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Public, NASDAQ GH. Q2 2026 total revenue was $335.0M, up 44% year on year. Full-year 2026 guidance was raised to $1.30-1.32B. An earlier 2026 forecast of $162-174M of screening revenue on 210,000-225,000 tests predates the guideline inclusion and the Quest arrangement, so it understates the current trajectory.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Not enumerated, and not in Massachusetts. A company tripling a screening business hires heavily in assay development, bioinformatics and commercial operations, but in California or remotely.

ASSESSMENT

On the numbers this is the healthiest diagnostics entry in the file: 44% revenue growth, guidance raised, a screening product in national guidelines with a major payer covering it, and an MRD business doubling. Guideline inclusion is the single hardest thing to achieve in diagnostics, and it is the reason to take this entry seriously despite the location. It is also winning its litigation posture against Tempus, which means its patent estate is an asset rather than a liability. Remote-or-relocate only, so weigh it against the Bay Area constraint in 02.
