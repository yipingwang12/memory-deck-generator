Sector: AI-native TechBio
Location: Cambridge, MA
Summary: Flagship's "scientific superintelligence" and autonomous-lab company.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2023 inside Flagship Pioneering; emerged from stealth early 2025. Co-founder and CEO Geoffrey von Maltzahn — also founding CEO of Generate, Tessera, Quotient Therapeutics and Indigo Ag, and co-founder of Sana Biotechnology and Seres Therapeutics. Co-founder and President of Future Science is Molly Gibson, previously Principal and Senior Principal at Flagship.

PLATFORM

An AI research engine uniting generative models, robotics and automated laboratories to run experiments autonomously across life, chemical and materials science. Not a drug company; an AI-for-science company situated in biotech. Domains span therapeutics, advanced materials, chemicals, energy and aerospace.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- No therapeutic pipeline in the conventional sense — the platform itself is the product.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Approximately $550M total: $200M seed, ~$235M Series A, and a ~$115M extension in Oct 2025. Valuation reported above $1.3B. Investors include Flagship, General Catalyst, March Capital, the ARK Venture Fund, Altitude Life Science Ventures, Blue Horizon Advisors, the State of Michigan Retirement System, Modi Ventures, NVIDIA and a wholly owned subsidiary of the Abu Dhabi Investment Authority.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Signed a 235,500 sq ft Cambridge lab lease, among the largest of the year — the strongest physical proxy for hiring intent in this file. Leadership is unusually AI-weighted: Andrew Beam (CTO and Head of AI Research), George Church (Chief Scientist), Jake Feala (SVP Autonomous Platform), Rafael Gomez-Bombarelli (CSO of Materials), John Gregoire (SVP Materials Sciences), Kenneth Stanley (SVP of Open-endedness), Jon Hennek (Chief Product Officer). Recruits "scientists and engineers, technologists and experimentalists".

ASSESSMENT

The most aggressive hiring story in Boston AI-bio. Would grow the ML-build edge maximally and move the career furthest from genomics. Kenneth Stanley's presence (open-endedness, novelty search) signals this is a genuine ML research organisation, not an applied group.
