HISTORY AND PEOPLE

A French pharmaceutical company whose US research presence is concentrated at Cambridge Crossing, the same development that holds Bristol Myers Squibb's research centre. The two entries together make Cambridge Crossing the densest large-pharma research cluster in this file after Kendall Square.

PLATFORM

Broad portfolio, with immunology as the commercial centre. The Blueprint acquisition adds precision kinase inhibition against genomically defined disease, which is the part closest to a genomics profile.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Broad portfolio; immunology-led.
- Blueprint Medicines assets, acquired July 2025 for $9.1B plus $400M in earnouts, are being integrated. Blueprint has its own retrospective entry in this file.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Public. The Cambridge Crossing footprint is about 900,000 sq ft across two buildings, built for roughly 2,500 to 2,700 employees. That is among the largest committed research footprints in the state.

TEAMS AND ROLES

The contradiction is the point. Sanofi holds space for 2,500-2,700 people at Cambridge Crossing while eliminating 229 legacy Blueprint positions between October 2026 and June 2027 and closing two Cambridge offices. No computational posting was enumerated this pass.

ASSESSMENT

A large, stable footprint with an active integration going badly for the acquired staff. The useful framing is that Sanofi's own Cambridge Crossing presence is not shrinking; what is shrinking is Blueprint. For a search, that means Sanofi is worth approaching directly on its own requisitions and worth avoiding as a destination for former Blueprint teams. It also means 229 experienced Cambridge people enter the market through mid-2027, which affects how competitive senior roles will be across this whole file.
