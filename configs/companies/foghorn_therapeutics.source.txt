Sector: Genomic medicine
Location: Cambridge, MA
Summary: Chromatin regulation drugs; the Lilly partnership appears to have just ended.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Built on the chromatin regulatory system: the machinery that controls which genes are accessible, rather than the genes themselves. This file noted the entry as "close to the epigenomics side of the CV", and that remains the most relevant point — of all the promoted genomic-medicine names this is the one whose biology is nearest to a genomics profile.

PLATFORM

Selective targeting of chromatin regulatory complexes, plus an induced proximity platform. Induced proximity means bringing two proteins together deliberately, which is the mechanism behind targeted protein degradation. The company's assets are mostly degraders.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- FHD-909 (LY4050784) — a selective SMARCA2 programme partnered with Lilly under a 50/50 US co-development and co-commercialisation agreement, first targeting SMARCA4-mutant non-small-cell lung cancer.
- UNRESOLVED: in August 2026 the company described the FHD-909 trial as advancing through dose escalation. An October 2026 report states that, after reviewing Phase 1 data, Foghorn and Lilly decided not to advance FHD-909 into clinical expansion, dropped the selective SMARCA2 degrader programme, and expect no further collaboration activities. The October report could not be confirmed against a company release or filing this pass. Check before relying on either version.
- Selective EP300 degrader — showed improved safety and efficacy against clinical benchmarks in multiple myeloma models.
- Selective CBP degrader — strong anti-tumour activity and tolerability in heavily pretreated ER-positive breast cancer models. Guidance for the CBPd-171 programme was pending because of third-party operational delays.
- An undisclosed oral immunology and inflammation programme, with an IND targeted for 2027.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Public, NASDAQ FHTX. An equity financing closed in January 2026. If the Lilly collaboration has indeed ended, the 50/50 cost share ends with it, which makes that January financing more important than it looked at the time.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Not enumerated this pass. Chromatin work is genomics-adjacent and usually involves ATAC-seq, ChIP-seq and related assays, so the computational content here should be closer to the current CV than anything else in this group. No posting was confirmed.

ASSESSMENT

The best latent domain fit among the promoted genomic-medicine names, and the one worth actually watching. Chromatin regulation sits beside the epigenomics side of the CV, and the assay stack is familiar. The problem is timing. The partnered lead programme may have just been discontinued, the company is pivoting to wholly owned assets with INDs targeted in 2027, and one programme's guidance is already delayed by a third party. Establish whether the Lilly collaboration is over before you spend effort. If it is, this becomes a single-financing company with no partner and a 2027 clinical horizon.

KEY FACTS

- Cambridge; drugs the chromatin regulatory system
- Has an induced proximity platform; its assets are mostly degraders
- FHD-909 targeted SMARCA2, partnered with Lilly for SMARCA4-mutant NSCLC
- Wholly owned programmes include selective EP300 and CBP degraders
