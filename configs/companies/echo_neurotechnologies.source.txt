Sector: Neurotech - speech BCI
Location: San Francisco Bay Area, CA
Summary: Speech-restoring BCI startup from UCSF neurosurgeon Edward Chang.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded and led by Edward Chang, chair of neurosurgery at UCSF. The company builds BCIs to restore communication for people with severe neurological disabilities. Largely in stealth.

PLATFORM

Not disclosed. Chang's lab is behind the 2023 study in which an implant plus AI let a paralysed woman speak through an avatar, and the 2025 UCSF/Berkeley streaming brain-to-voice method. Those are lab results, not company results.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- June 2026: Intel strategic partnership to develop algorithms trained on brain activity, disclosed by Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan at Computex 2026.
- One aggregator lists a planned wireless implant trial; not confirmed.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

About $50M, January 2025, backed by Andreessen Horowitz (Bloomberg). Other investor names are unconfirmed.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Greenhouse board shows hardware and manufacturing engineering roles. Not researched further.

ASSESSMENT

Thin entry by necessity: the company is in stealth. Speech decoding is the BCI application where ML is most central, so it is the closest neurotech match to the ML-build goal, but nothing is public enough to assess. Watch.
