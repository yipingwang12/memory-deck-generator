HISTORY AND PEOPLE

A Harvard-affiliated psychiatric hospital in Belmont, and the home of the Institute for Technology in Psychiatry. It is the most developed computational psychiatry environment in the Boston area, which makes 03's finding about it the more significant.

PLATFORM

Digital phenotyping: deriving clinical measures from passively collected data rather than from questionnaires. Justin Baker co-founded the Institute for Technology in Psychiatry and is its scientific director; his work builds computational phenotypes from audiovisual recordings, electronic health record data, wearables and smartphones. Diego Pizzagalli directs the Laboratory for Translational and Affective Neuroscience.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS
