HISTORY AND PEOPLE

A Cambridge research institute, affiliated with MIT but independently governed, and explicitly oriented toward translation rather than publication alone. Ruth Lehmann has been Director since July 2020, succeeding David C. Page, and also serves as President on the Board of Directors. The row was `carried` with nine words; it is now `verified`.

PLATFORM

Basic molecular and cellular biology with a translational intent. The institute's model is small numbers of independent Members with broad latitude, rather than programme-organised research — the opposite structure from the Broad, which is organised into platforms.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- David Bartel is a Member, listed in the institute's June 2026 faculty brochure. His cited contribution is the finding that microRNAs affect most human protein-coding genes.
