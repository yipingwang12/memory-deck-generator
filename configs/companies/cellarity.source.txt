HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Flagship Pioneering started Cellarity in Flagship Labs in 2017. Avak Kahvejian, a Flagship General Partner, was the founding CEO. Nick Plugis, a Flagship Senior Principal, co-founded the company with him. Noubar Afeyan, the founder of Flagship, is also a co-founder. Jim Collins is the academic co-founder. He is the Termeer Professor at MIT and holds positions at the Broad Institute and the Wyss Institute. Fabrice Chouraqui served as CEO-Partner. Ted Myles became CEO in May 2025.

PLATFORM

Cellarity acts on the behaviour of a cell. It does not act on one molecular target. The platform maps networks of cell and gene interactions. It uses single-cell transcriptomics, other omics data and AI models. From these maps it designs oral small molecules that correct a cell state. The company calls these Cell State-Correcting therapies. The target mechanism is a gene switch. 02 describes the platform as Miraldi-style methods made into a drug company.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- CLY-124 — lead asset. A potential first-in-class small molecule that switches globin genes for sickle cell disease. Phase 1 proof-of-concept started 2025. 28-day data due Q4 2026.
- CLY-124 — multiple ascending dose cohorts planned for the US, Kenya and other countries.
- Myelofibrosis — preclinical. In vivo data against the standard of care due Q2 2026. Development candidate nomination due Q4 2026.
- MASH — discovery and development collaboration with Novo Nordisk.
- IBD — a second discovery and development collaboration, named in the January 2026 outlook.
