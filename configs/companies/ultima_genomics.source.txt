Location: California; remote roles available
Summary: Cheap sequencing at scale, and the file's only verified remote comp band.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

A California sequencing company competing on cost per genome rather than on read length or accuracy alone. Its argument is that if sequencing becomes cheap enough, experimental designs change — which is the same argument that makes it interesting to a methods person.

PLATFORM

High-throughput sequencing on a wafer-based architecture, with the Solaris chemistry and workflow stack layered on top. Output per wafer is the metric the company competes on.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- UG 100 — the first-generation system.
- UG200 Series launched at AGBT on 25 February 2026, with Solaris 2.0 workflows. Pricing starts at about $850,000 for the single-wafer UG200, and $1.25M for the dual-wafer UG200 Ultra.
- Shipments were scheduled to begin in Q2 2026, after an early access programme.
- Solaris 2.0 Max is scheduled for H2 2026 and would roughly double output, to up to 20 billion reads per wafer.
- Existing UG 100 instruments can be upgraded in the field to UG 100 Plus configurations, so the installed base is not stranded.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Private. No 2026 funding round was found this pass, and no revenue figure is public. Instrument pricing is the only hard commercial number available.
