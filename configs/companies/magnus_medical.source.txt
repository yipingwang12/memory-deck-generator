Sector: Neurotech - accelerated TMS for depression
Location: Burlingame, CA
Summary: SAINT, a five-day, MRI-targeted TMS treatment for treatment-resistant depression.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Launched October 2021 with a $25M Series A and an FDA Breakthrough Device designation for SAINT. SAINT was developed by Nolan Williams at Stanford; Magnus holds an exclusive licence to Stanford's patents. A February 2026 company release refers to "the late" Nolan Williams. CEO: Christian Gormsen.

PLATFORM

SAINT neuromodulation: functional and structural MRI pick an individual stimulation target in each patient, then accelerated intermittent theta-burst stimulation (iTBS) runs 10 sessions a day, about 10 minutes each, hourly, for 5 days: 50 sessions in total instead of the usual six weeks. Hardware partner since 2024: MagVenture.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- FDA 510(k) K220177, cleared 1 September 2022, for adults with major depression who did not respond to antidepressants.
- Double-blind randomized trial: 79% remission with SAINT vs 13% with sham.
- Commercial launch 30 April 2024.
- May 2026: programs at Cleveland Clinic, UPMC and HCA, in 14 states; coverage of more than 80 million lives (company claim, volatile).
- Postpartum depression: DoD-funded multisite pivotal study; first patient enrolled at UMass, February 2026.
- CMS: 2025 hospital payment of $19,703 for the full protocol; four Category III CPT codes (0889T-0892T) kept for 2026. Company calls SAINT the first mental-health therapy with CMS new-technology payment support.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

$25M Series A at launch (October 2021). No later round was found.

ASSESSMENT

The clearest commercial success in psychiatric neuromodulation, and a direct overlap with Shaohuan's computational-psychiatry field: the MRI targeting step is a neuroimaging pipeline. The founder's death and the cease-and-desist letters in 2026 suggest the company is now defending its protocol against copies. California.
