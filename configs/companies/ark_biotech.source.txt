Sector: Adjacent - cultivated meat infrastructure
Location: Boston, MA
Summary: Airlift bioreactors for cultivated meat; quiet since 2024 in a contracting sector.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

A Boston company founded by Yossi Quint, a former McKinsey analyst, to build the manufacturing infrastructure for cultivated meat rather than the product itself. The thesis is that the sector's binding constraint is cost per litre of bioreactor capacity, not biology. Primary Venture Partners backed it from the seed round. One directory gives 2021 as the founding year.

PLATFORM

Large-volume bioreactors designed for animal cell culture. The design uses vertical tubes and airlift technology to mix contents uniformly, with high mass transfer, without the impeller of a conventional stirred tank. Quint argues that impellers become an energy bottleneck at large sizes. To scale up, the user adds more tubes. The company claims that at scale its bioreactor is 90% cheaper than the biggest bioreactor in the world today; that is a company figure. Its control software works as a digital twin: users can see what is happening, simulate scenarios and adjust more than 15 parameters in real time.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Bioreactor hardware and control systems, sold to cultivated meat producers.
- In late 2023 Ark ran a 1,250-litre bioreactor with a cultivated meat company.
- Reporting from around 2023 described a planned upgraded bioreactor and a new controller. No confirmation that these shipped was found.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS
