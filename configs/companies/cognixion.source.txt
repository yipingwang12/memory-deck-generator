Sector: Neurotech - assistive BCI
Location: Santa Barbara, CA
Summary: EEG BCI inside an AR headset for late-stage ALS, running on Apple Vision Pro.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

A Santa Barbara company building communication technology for people who cannot speak. The target population is specific and the need is real: conversational-speed communication in late-stage ALS, where existing eye-tracking systems are slow and fail as the disease progresses.

PLATFORM

Axon-R is the research version of the Cognixion One headset. It combines an EEG brain-computer interface with an augmented-reality display and an AI application that predicts and completes intended speech. The notable integration detail: the trial runs the Axon-R with the Apple Vision Pro, which puts this entry alongside Synchron as one of two companies in this file working through Apple hardware.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- The registered study is NCT06810219, 'Augmented Reality BCI Longitudinal Study for Persons with Late Stage ALS'. It launched in January 2025 and is enrolling in the US.
- CORRECTION: that study assesses safety, tolerability and feasibility. This file previously described a pivotal trial planned for 2026. The registered trial is a feasibility study, not a pivotal one, so the regulatory timeline in the old row was optimistic.
- The Cognixion One headset received an FDA breakthrough device designation in 2023.
- A 2026 article reports the company hoping for FDA approval later that year, but its date could not be established, so treat the claim as possibly stale.
- Clinicians and patients give input on the system design during the study.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

About $25M raised, with Prime Movers Lab and the Amazon Alexa Fund among the investors. No funding information appeared in the sources found this pass, so that figure is carried from the previous row rather than re-verified.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Not enumerated. The computational content is EEG decoding plus language modelling for predictive communication, which is a genuinely interesting combination and closer to a methods role than most entries at this fit level.

ASSESSMENT

The most clearly beneficial product in the neurotech group, and the clearest case of an entry whose stage this file had overstated. A feasibility study enrolling since January 2025 is several years from approval, whatever the company hopes. The work itself — EEG decoding feeding a language model, delivered through consumer AR hardware — is technically appealing and the assistive mission is unambiguous. Santa Barbara rules it out on geography. Watch for the feasibility results, which would be the first real evidence either way.
