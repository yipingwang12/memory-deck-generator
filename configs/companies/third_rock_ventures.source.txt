HISTORY AND PEOPLE

A Boston venture-creation firm. It does not primarily back existing companies; it forms them, staffing them from its own ranks. 01 notes that it deliberately creates serial operators, which is the reason it appears in a career dossier at all: the firm is a factory for the kind of operating experience that compounds.

PLATFORM

Company formation. Scientific ideas are developed in-house and then spun into new companies, with firm partners taking founding roles. Compare Flagship Pioneering in this file, which uses the same model and produced Generate, Cellarity, Lila, Tessera, Moderna and Seaport. Third Rock is the second-largest instance of the model in Boston.
