Sector: Neurotech - deep TMS
Location: Jerusalem, Israel, and Burlington, MA
Summary: Deep TMS with H-coils for depression, OCD and smoking cessation; a US office near Boston.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founding year is 2003 (Wikipedia) or 2006 (Renaissance Capital). Wikipedia says the H-coil came from NIH research in the late 1990s and early 2000s by Abraham Zangen, Roy Wise and Mark Hallett (one source). Listed in Tel Aviv from 2007 (one source). CEO: Hadar Levy.

PLATFORM

Deep TMS: H-shaped coils that reach deeper brain regions than the figure-8 coils of standard TMS. Different coils target different circuits: H1 for depression, H7 for OCD and also depression, H4 for smoking.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Major depression: FDA clearance 2013 (H1 coil).
- OCD: FDA De Novo 2018 (H7 coil); company calls it the first non-invasive device cleared for OCD.
- Smoking cessation: FDA clearance August 2020; company calls it the first TMS clearance in addiction.
- Anxious depression: 2021. H7 coil for depression: August 2022.
- Late-life depression: company says it is the only TMS system cleared for ages 68 to 86.
- Accelerated protocol: FDA clearance 16 September 2025. Acute phase of 6 treatment days instead of 4 weeks; iTBS; median time to remission 21 days vs 28 with the standard protocol.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Nasdaq IPO April 2019, ticker BWAY; Cantor Fitzgerald sole bookrunner. Price is given as $11.00 or $11.94. A 2011 Nasdaq attempt was withdrawn.
