HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Harvard Medical School affiliate. Todd Golub founded the Broad's Cancer Program at Dana-Farber and co-founded Foundation Medicine; Levi Garraway was a Dana-Farber medical oncologist. The institutional overlap with the Broad is close. Sidney Farber, a pediatric pathologist, founded the institute in 1947 as the Children's Cancer Research Foundation. The Jimmy Fund began in 1948 with a radio broadcast from the bedside of a young patient called "Jimmy", and the Boston Red Sox adopted it as their official charity in 1953. The institute took patients of all ages from 1969. Its present name, from 1983, honours the long support of the Charles A. Dana Foundation. Benjamin Ebert became President and CEO on 1 October 2024, succeeding Laurie Glimcher; he previously chaired the Department of Medical Oncology. William Kaelin, a Dana-Farber professor of medicine, shared the 2019 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for discovering how cells sense and adapt to oxygen. Dana-Farber's clinical affiliation with Brigham and Women's Hospital began in 1997 and ends in 2028. In September 2023 Dana-Farber announced a new partnership with Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center. In March 2025 the state Public Health Council approved its plan for a new cancer hospital at 1 Joslin Place, with 300 adult inpatient beds.

PLATFORM

The Department of Data Science is chaired by Rafael Irizarry, who is also Professor of Biostatistics at the Harvard T.H. Chan School and the Lavine Family Chair for Preventative Cancer Therapies. He came from Johns Hopkins Biostatistics; his PhD, from Berkeley, was on statistical models for music sound signals. The department spans clinical trial design, computational biology, statistical genomics and machine learning.
