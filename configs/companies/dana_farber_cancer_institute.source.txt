Sector: Academic and non-profit
Location: 450 Brookline Ave, Boston (Longwood)
Summary: Roughly 30 open comp-bio positions; a real Department of Data Science.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Harvard Medical School affiliate. Todd Golub founded the Broad's Cancer Program at Dana-Farber and co-founded Foundation Medicine; Levi Garraway was a Dana-Farber medical oncologist. The institutional overlap with the Broad is close.

PLATFORM

The Department of Data Science is chaired by Rafael Irizarry, who is also Professor of Biostatistics at the Harvard T.H. Chan School and the Lavine Family Chair for Preventative Cancer Therapies. He came from Johns Hopkins Biostatistics; his PhD, from Berkeley, was on statistical models for music sound signals. The department spans clinical trial design, computational biology, statistical genomics and machine learning.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Cancer genomics, computational pediatric cancer research (Gillani Lab), cancer screening data science.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Grant and philanthropy funded.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Approximately 30 open bioinformatics and computational biology positions. Seen this pass: staff Data Scientist roles in the Department of Data Science requiring strong scientific programming and collaboration with basic and clinical researchers to develop new statistical methods and analysis pipelines; a Postdoctoral Fellow in Data Science for Cancer Screening Research; a Post-Doctoral Computational Biologist in the Gillani Lab; and a faculty search for an Assistant or Associate Professor of Cancer Data Science jointly with Harvard Medical School. Note the postdoc band is $72,000-$76,385, far below the staff track — the tracks are not interchangeable and the distinction matters for 04's affordability model.

ASSESSMENT

Direct cancer-genomics match with the largest count of actual open roles found anywhere this pass. Mind which track: staff scientist, not postdoc.
