Sector: Neurotech - surface BCI
Location: New York, NY
Summary: Thin-film brain-surface array; first modern BCI with FDA 510(k) clearance.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2021 by Benjamin Rapoport (Mount Sinai neurosurgeon; MIT EECS PhD, Harvard MD; on Neuralink's original eight-member founding team) and Michael Mager (former private equity). Offices in Santa Clara, CA and Addison, TX.

PLATFORM

Layer 7 Cortical Interface: 1,024 microelectrodes in a film thinner than a hair that sits on the brain surface without penetrating it; inserted through a thin slit.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- FDA 510(k) clearance April 2025 for recording, monitoring and stimulation up to 30 days.
- After clearance, extended-duration implantation studies opened at six medical centres. Patients recovering from neurosurgery use the device for up to 30 days on BCI tasks.
- Record: 4,096 electrodes in one human procedure (Mount Sinai, May 2024).
- More than 100 patient procedures have now used the technology, across 18 medical institutions. That supersedes the ~27 figure previously held here.
- A partnership with Medtronic integrates the technology with an established neurosurgical platform.
- Work at the University of Pennsylvania used high-resolution recordings from the interface to build a refined map of the human motor cortex.
- In development: a fully implantable, wireless, chronic system for people with severe paralysis from spinal-cord injury, stroke or ALS.
- John Donoghue (BrainGate pioneer) appointed scientific advisor, July 2026.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

$102M Series C (Dec 2024) at ~$500M post-money. An oversubscribed $250M Series D followed, reported around 25 September 2026. Total capital raised since the 2021 founding is about $430M. This file previously held ~$180M, so the figure has more than doubled. Stated uses are clinical expansion, further FDA review of Layer 7, and commercialisation infrastructure. One source dates the round only relatively, so treat late September 2026 as approximate.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Neural data, clinical, hardware. No individual posting was confirmed this pass. A company that has just raised $250M for clinical expansion and commercialisation will be hiring, and the Penn motor-cortex work shows that high-resolution neural data analysis happens around this platform.

ASSESSMENT

A regulatory leader, and now one of the best-funded companies in the field. Three things changed since this entry was written. Funding went from about $180M to about $430M. Patient exposure went from about 27 to more than 100 across 18 institutions. And a Medtronic partnership gives it a route through an existing surgical platform rather than a standalone launch. The Donoghue appointment links it to the Boston BrainGate lineage, but the company is in New York with offices in Santa Clara and Addison, not in Boston.
