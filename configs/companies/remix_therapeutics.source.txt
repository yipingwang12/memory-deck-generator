Summary: RNA-processing small molecules; merging with Passage Bio to list as RMTX.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Launched in December 2020 with $81M. The company designs small molecules that reprogram how RNA is processed and spliced, rather than targeting a protein directly. This file previously recorded Cambridge as its location and marked the entry `carried`. Both are now corrected: the company is in Watertown, and the facts below come from SEC filings, so the entry is `verified`.

PLATFORM

Small-molecule modulation of RNA processing and splicing. The mechanism allows degradation of targets that are hard to drug by conventional means, because the intervention happens while the transcript is still being processed. The lead programme is a degrader, so the molecule removes the protein rather than inhibiting it.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- REM-422 — lead asset, an oral MYB-targeting degrader. In a Phase 2 study in adenoid cystic carcinoma after end-of-Phase-1 alignment with the FDA. The agency allowed a single-arm design that could support registration, which is an unusually favourable path.
- A MYB poison exon is in development as a possible companion diagnostic, with Tempus AI. Tempus has its own entry in this file.
- REM-422 holds Fast Track and Orphan Drug designations in adenoid cystic carcinoma.
- A Phase 1 in relapsed or refractory AML and high-risk MDS is running. Recommended Phase 2 dose and top-line data are expected around mid-2027.
- Preclinical: a programme against MYC-dysregulated cancers, a driver implicated in roughly 25% of human malignancies.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

About $201M raised privately, including a $60M financing in January 2024. Two partnerships sit behind that: a 2022 Janssen deal worth $45M up front with roughly $1B of upside, which is the Johnson & Johnson link, and a January 2024 Roche collaboration worth up to $1B in milestones plus tiered royalties. In June 2026 Remix agreed an all-stock combination with Passage Bio. The combined company trades as RMTX, and a roughly $100M private placement led by Decheng Capital is expected to fund operations into 2028.

ASSESSMENT

The strongest of the promoted AI-native group on substance. A registrational single-arm path agreed with the FDA, two large pharma partnerships, and funding into 2028 through the merger. The Tempus AI companion-diagnostic work is the most directly relevant detail for this profile: poison-exon detection is transcript-level analysis of clinical samples, which is close to the current DGG work.
