HISTORY AND PEOPLE

The Gene Lay Institute of Immunology and Inflammation, established with a $100M gift from Gene Lay, the founder and CEO of BioLegend, through the Laygend Foundation. It was announced in May 2023 and is the largest gift in Brigham and Women's Hospital's history. It is a joint institute of Brigham and Women's Hospital, Massachusetts General Hospital and Harvard Medical School. Dana-Farber and the Broad Institute are involved in some projects, which connects it to three other entries in this file. The row was `carried`; it is now `verified`.

PLATFORM

Immunology and inflammation, with stated research areas of immune-mediated disease, aging and cancer, aimed at new immunotherapies. The computational relevance is high because modern immunology is a single-cell discipline — the same methods as the CV, applied to immune rather than tumour populations.
