Sector: Neurotech - endovascular BCI
Location: Brooklyn, NY
Summary: Brain interface delivered through a blood vessel; Apple-integrated.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2016 from the University of Melbourne by Tom Oxley (CEO; vascular and interventional neurologist), Nicholas Opie (CTO; biomedical engineer who invented the Stentrode) and cardiologist Rahul Sharma. Now New York based.

PLATFORM

Stentrode: a stent-mounted electrode array placed in a vein beside the motor cortex through the jugular vein, avoiding open brain surgery. The trade-off is channel count. The first-generation Stentrode carries 16 electrodes. Neuralink's N1 carries 1,024. Synchron buys a far easier surgical route at a large cost in bandwidth, and that single comparison explains most of the strategic difference between the two companies.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- COMMAND early feasibility study (6 patients; FDA Breakthrough). The FDA granted its IDE in July 2021 — the first IDE the agency had ever granted for a permanently implanted BCI.
- Pivotal trial 2026 for what could be the first FDA-approved BCI. The company must clear it before it can seek premarket approval. This is a stated plan, not a milestone reached.
- First native integration with Apple's BCI-HID profile, which Apple unveiled on 13 May 2025 and which makes neural input a native input category beside touch and voice. Synchron said controlled rollouts with trial participants would begin later in 2025; no confirmation was found.
- Nvidia: the company used the Holoscan platform from January 2025 for on-device edge neural processing.
- Chiral: announced at Nvidia GTC in March 2025 as a roadmap toward a foundation model of human cognition, in three stages, starting with motor inference on Holoscan. Treat Chiral as a roadmap. It was not a released model at announcement.
- Next-generation device in development.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

$200M Series D announced 6 November 2025, led by Double Point Ventures; $345M total. The round was raised specifically to fund the pivotal trial and to prepare the first-generation device for commercial launch.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Clinical, regulatory, neural decoding, software. The Chiral roadmap describes training models on human neural activity, which is the most ML-forward programme of any implant BCI company. No specific posting was confirmed.

ASSESSMENT

The likeliest first approved BCI, and the Apple protocol makes it the most "integrated with consumer tech". New York, not Boston. Separate the shipped facts from the announced ones: the Apple profile integration and the Holoscan work are real, while Chiral and the pivotal approval are plans. A 16-electrode device is also a thin signal source for a foundation model, so treat the Chiral framing with care.

KEY FACTS

- Founded 2016 by Tom Oxley and Nicholas Opie, University of Melbourne
- Device: Stentrode, placed through a blood vessel
- First BCI natively integrated with Apple's BCI-HID protocol
- First-generation Stentrode has 16 electrodes
- COMMAND had the first FDA IDE ever granted for a permanently implanted BCI
- Chiral is its proposed foundation model of human cognition
