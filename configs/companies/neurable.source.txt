Sector: Neurotech - noninvasive EEG
Location: Boston, MA
Summary: EEG built into everyday headphones; a University of Michigan spinout.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2015-16 as a University of Michigan spinout via U-M Innovation Partnerships. Core technology came from U-M's Direct Brain Interface laboratory, starting in 2011 with an AI method to raise the signal-to-noise ratio of EEG. Co-founded by four U-M student researchers: Ramses Alcaide (CEO; PhD in electrical engineering and neuroscience, thesis on BCIs and EEG biomarkers in children with cerebral palsy), Michael Thompson, James Hamet and Adam Molnar.

PLATFORM

"Neurable AI": signal processing and ML that make a few dry EEG sensors in ear cushions usable outside the lab. Reports focus, cognitive speed, cognitive strain, mental recovery and "brain age".

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- MW75 Neuro headphones with Master & Dynamic — the first commercial product.
- OEM licensing: plans to launch with five global headwear makers 2026-2028.
- Longevity-clinic channel via Healthspan Digital.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

$35M Series A on 19 Dec 2025; ~$56.6M total across 7 rounds from 17 investors. Epson is a strategic investor (2023).

TEAMS AND ROLES

~39 employees (May 2026). Has posted ML scientist/researcher roles: new algorithms for speed, accuracy and muscle-artifact removal in EEG. Also data scientist postings.

ASSESSMENT

Real product and fresh capital, but small and moving to a licensing model, so headcount growth will be modest. It measures cognitive state; it does not extend it. Work is EEG signal processing, far from genomics. A useful Ann Arbor warm link: same U-M spinout pipeline flagged in 01.

KEY FACTS

- University of Michigan spinout; tech from U-M's Direct Brain Interface lab
- CEO and co-founder Ramses Alcaide
- EEG in Master & Dynamic MW75 Neuro headphones
- Business model shifting to OEM licensing of Neurable AI
