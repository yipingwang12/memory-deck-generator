Sector: Established biopharma
Location: Cambridge, MA (Kendall Square)
Summary: Explicitly hiring AI and data science on multi-omic biobank data.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Novartis first opened labs in Cambridge in 2003, in the former NECCO candy factory. The research organisation was established as the Novartis Institutes for BioMedical Research (NIBR), initially led by Mark Fishman, previously chief of cardiology and director of cardiovascular research at Massachusetts General Hospital and a Harvard Medical School professor. Now branded Biomedical Research.

PLATFORM

A 500,000 sq ft research centre in the heart of Kendall Square, positioned for collaboration with the surrounding academic and biotech ecosystem.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Broad therapeutic portfolio; the relevant unit is Discovery Sciences and its Data Science team.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Corporate. Caution: Novartis has broken ground on a new global Biomedical Research centre in San Diego, so the internal centre of gravity may be shifting west.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Approximately 2,300 scientists, clinicians and business professionals, making Novartis one of the largest corporate employers in Cambridge. Specific openings seen this pass: Data Scientist for AI in Biomedical Imaging within Discovery Sciences; Associate Director, Data Science, advancing data-driven discovery by integrating advanced analytics, machine learning and mechanistic modelling; and a Data Science and AI Innovation Postdoctoral Fellow developing AI methods to integrate ECG, genomic and proteomic data at scale across population biobanks, clinical studies and real-world data. The 2026 postdoctoral cohort starts 1 October 2026.

ASSESSMENT

The multi-omic integration fellowship is almost a description of the profile, and a postdoctoral fellowship inside a pharma research organisation is an unusual side door that would close the keystone on someone else's payroll. The San Diego build is the thing to ask about.

KEY FACTS

- Opened Cambridge labs in 2003 in the former NECCO candy factory
- Around 2,300 people; 500,000 sq ft in Kendall Square
- Research arm was the Novartis Institutes for BioMedical Research, NIBR
- First led by Mark Fishman, previously of MGH cardiology
