Summary: Tufts cellular agriculture centre whose five-year federal anchor grant has just run out.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

The Tufts University Center for Cellular Agriculture, in Medford. It hosts the National Institute for Cellular Agriculture, established with a USDA award announced on 6 October 2021. David Kaplan leads it. The institute is a consortium: investigators from Virginia Tech, Virginia State, UC Davis, MIT and UMass Boston are involved alongside Tufts.

PLATFORM

Cellular agriculture: growing animal cells for food rather than farming animals. The research spans cell lines, growth media, scaffolds and scale-up. It is a tissue-engineering discipline, so the computational content is modest.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- The USDA award was $10M over five years, from the USDA-NIFA Sustainable Agricultural Systems programme, within a $146M round. It was the first US public investment in cultivated protein research.
- A NASA-linked grant through Baylor's TRISH programme runs 2024-2026 with Kaplan as a team member.
- Other funding comes from industry Sponsored Research Agreements, New Harvest graduate fellowships, Good Food Institute grants and private donations.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS
