Sector: Neurotech - ultrasound neuromodulation
Location: San Francisco, CA
Summary: Noninvasive focused ultrasound to reach deep brain regions.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2024 in San Francisco by Fred Ehrsam (Coinbase co-founder) and Quintin Frerichs. Jeremy Barenholtz (ex-Neuralink) is President and CTO.

PLATFORM

Nudge Zero, unveiled in April 2025: an MRI-compatible helmet delivering low-intensity transcranial focused ultrasound to millimetre-scale deep brain targets without surgery. The Focused Ultrasound Foundation notes that the FDA has approved no low-intensity focused ultrasound device. The stated path is medical conditions first, then a smaller headphone-like version for mood and cognition in healthy people.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Essential tremor study sponsored by Nudge; studies in development for chronic pain, tinnitus and substance use disorder.
- The essential tremor study enrols up to 150 adults at a single San Francisco site. David Segar, a functional neurosurgeon, is the principal investigator.
- Sessions take place inside an MRI scanner. Participants attend multiple focused-ultrasound sessions over about three weeks, each lasting two to three hours.
- Targets are the VIM thalamus, and possibly the dentatorubrothalamic tract. MRI guidance shows the target in real time.
- The study is not designed to demonstrate safety or efficacy. It gathers preliminary data to guide later clinical development.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

$100M Series A announced July 2025, led by Thrive Capital and Greenoaks, with several angel investors. Goodwin advised on the financing. No valuation was disclosed.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Acoustic modelling, neuroimaging, clinical research. No posting was enumerated this pass. The MRI-guided design means real-time image-based targeting, which is closer to medical imaging work than to genomics.

ASSESSMENT

Big raise for an early approach. Ultrasound is the modality both Nudge (write) and Merge Labs (read) are betting on. California. The gap between the raise and the evidence is the thing to hold on to: $100M has bought a 150-person single-site study that is explicitly not powered for safety or efficacy. A two-to-three-hour session inside an MRI scanner is also not a consumer product, so any route to scale is years away and depends on moving the stimulation out of the scanner.
