HISTORY AND PEOPLE

The Kempner Institute for the Study of Natural and Artificial Intelligence, at Harvard, in Allston. It is the Boston institution most explicitly organised around brain-AI convergence rather than around either field separately. Allston also puts it beside the Roche Genentech Innovation Center on Harvard's Enterprise Research Campus, so that corner of the city now has two of this file's strongest entries. The institute was created by a gift from the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative, announced in December 2021: $500M over 15 years. Its name honours Mark Zuckerberg's mother, Karen Kempner Zuckerberg, and her parents, Sidney and Gertrude Kempner. The gift funds ten new faculty appointments, new computing infrastructure and support for students. The institute has space in the Science and Engineering Complex in Allston. Its co-directors are Sham Kakade and Bernardo Sabatini.

PLATFORM

Theory and method rather than application: how natural intelligence works, how artificial intelligence works, and what each tells you about the other. For this profile the appeal is that it is a methods institution by design, so building models is the job rather than a means to a biological end.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Research Fellows — independent postdoctoral-level positions. Fellows get salary and research support, office space, compute resources and engineering support, and must propose two to three mentors from current faculty.
- ACTION: the Research Fellow deadline was 1 October 2026, for appointments starting September 2027. That date passed thirteen days before this pass. If an application was not filed, the next cycle is a year away.
- Kempner AI Fellows — for holders of a bachelor's or master's degree, working under a Kempner investigator. One-year appointments, renewable up to three consecutive years. The 2026 deadline of 1 June has also passed.
- The institute also recruits research engineers, and a Fellow posting in AI and ML for scientific applications, foundation models and AI systems was open in April 2026.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Harvard-funded and philanthropically endowed. The compute and engineering support offered to fellows is the material point: an academic post that comes with cluster access and engineers removes the usual constraint on doing serious model work inside a university. In October 2023 the institute bought 384 NVIDIA H100 GPUs. In March 2026 it added 424 H200 GPUs and 192 RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPUs, for 1,144 GPUs in total. The cluster's nodes sit at the Massachusetts Green High Performance Computing Center, in western Massachusetts.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Two named programmes plus research engineer roles. The structure requires naming faculty mentors, which means the application is really a request to work with specific people — so the preparation is identifying two or three Kempner investigators whose work genuinely overlaps with single-cell methods, and approaching them before the next deadline rather than at it.
