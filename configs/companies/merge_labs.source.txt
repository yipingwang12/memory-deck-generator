Sector: Neurotech - molecular noninvasive BCI
Location: SF Bay Area
Summary: Altman's BCI company; molecules and ultrasound instead of electrodes.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2025 by Sam Altman, Alex Blania (CEO of Tools for Humanity), Sandro Herbig (Tools for Humanity product and engineering), Mikhail Shapiro (Caltech; ultrasound and molecular bioengineering), and Tyson Aflalo and Sumner Norman (both from Forest Neurotech's ultrasound BCI work). Emerged from stealth 15 January 2026. The structural link to Forest is stronger than shared staff: Merge Labs is a spinoff of Forest Neurotech. Forest is a Los Angeles nonprofit formed in 2023, and it continues to operate independently while collaborating with the company. Forest has its own entry in this file, so read the two together. OpenAI states that Altman is involved in a personal capacity.

PLATFORM

Noninvasive, high-bandwidth BCI: "connect with neurons using molecules instead of electrodes" and transmit and receive through deep-reaching modalities like ultrasound. Implies genetically delivered biosensors read out by ultrasound. The company avoids implants into brain tissue. That is the deliberate break from Neuralink, Paradromics and Precision. The stated aims are high bandwidth, integration with advanced AI, and eventual safe and broad access. OpenAI is a research collaborator as well as an investor.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Early research; no human device.
- No specific product application has been disclosed. The OpenAI collaboration is framed as research, not as near-term commercial work.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

$252M seed at ~$850M valuation (Jan 2026): OpenAI, Bain Capital, Gabe Newell and others. Second only to Neuralink among US BCI efforts by capital raised. No later round was found in October 2026, which is normal nine months after a seed of this size.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Hiring ML research scientists; senior/principal ML engineers (infrastructure, CI/CD); developers of molecular-dynamics pipelines combining physics models with ML; protein engineers for biosensors; non-viral gene delivery scientists; research associates in molecular biology, neurobiology, immunotolerisation and in vivo work. Mostly Bay Area.

ASSESSMENT

The one BCI company whose core is molecular biology plus ML, so it is the closest industry fit to a comp-bio profile in the whole field. The blocker is geography: Shaohuan vetoed the Bay Area (02), and remote roles are unconfirmed. Two further cautions. The company has published no technical result since launch, so the science is still a thesis and not a demonstration. And it has disclosed no product, which means the first job here is research with no delivery date. Against that, the roles listed above are an unusually exact match, so this stays the best latent fit in the field even though the location rules it out today.
