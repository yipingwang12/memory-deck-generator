Sector: AI-native TechBio

Summary: AI-designed AAV capsids; the address is also a housing answer.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2018 by Eric Kelsic (CEO), Alan Crane, Adrian Veres, George Church, Sam Sinai and Tomas Bjorklund. Kelsic developed the underlying capsid-engineering technology in George Church's lab at the Wyss Institute, where he measured the first comprehensive fitness landscape of the AAV capsid protein and co-discovered the AAV MAAP gene. Church chairs the Scientific Advisory Board.

PLATFORM

Machine learning over billions of in vivo non-human-primate measurements to engineer AAV capsids optimised simultaneously for selective delivery, liver detargeting, cross-species translatability and manufacturability. Connects frontier AI agents and foundation models to capsid design.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Dyno-yp2 — CNS capsid, updated in vivo data May 2026
- Dyno-bn8 — muscle capsid, updated in vivo data May 2026
- Two further capsids for CNS and muscular delivery launched at ASGCT, May 2026
- Earlier: three capsids for eye, muscle and CNS launched at ASGCT 2025

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

$109M raised. Series A led by a16z in 2021 — no priced round since, which is five years. The platform is active and shipping; the balance sheet is the open question. Partnerships with Novartis, Sarepta, Roche, Spark and Astellas.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Approximately 75 employees. Small enough that a senior computational hire would be structurally significant.
