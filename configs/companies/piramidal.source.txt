Sector: Neurotech - EEG foundation models
Location: San Francisco, CA
Summary: Small YC company putting a foundation model on clinical EEG; Cleveland Clinic partner.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

A Y Combinator company, Winter 2024 batch, co-founded by Dimitris Fotis Sakellariou and Kris Pahuja. Sources give the founding year as either 2023 or 2024. The stated aim is accuracy and speed in EEG analysis, which is a narrower and more testable goal than most entries in this group set themselves.

PLATFORM

A foundation model for EEG. The clinical problem is concrete: reading clinical EEG is a specialist task measured in hours, and intensive-care monitoring generates more signal than neurologists can review. The company's claim is to compress that reading to seconds, for seizures and altered consciousness in particular. Compare Hemispheric in this file: same modality and same model class, but Hemispheric is building a diagnostic product on a proprietary consumer-grade dataset, while Piramidal is aimed at the hospital reading workflow.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- First product, as of March 2024, was described as an epilepsy diagnosis copilot powered by its brain model.
- A Cleveland Clinic partnership covers an AI foundation model that monitors patients' brain health in intensive care using EEG. It was reported in August 2025, and Cleveland Clinic described it again in April 2026.
- The ICU model looks for seizures, altered consciousness and declining brain function. Piramidal built it on public EEG datasets plus proprietary EEG data from the Cleveland Clinic and other partners.
- Manual review of a day of ICU EEG can take two to four hours, which is the delay the model targets.
- No FDA clearance, trial result or 2026 product news was found.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

A $6M seed in August 2024, led by Y Combinator with Adverb Ventures and Lionheart Ventures, plus angel investors including the founders of Intercom, PlanGrid and Guilded. No later round was found, so the company has been running on a $6M seed for over two years.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Small, and not enumerated. A company of this size building a foundation model is almost entirely ML engineers and clinical data people, so the work would be the right shape. The constraint is that there may be very few seats.

ASSESSMENT

The fit 3 rating is right on substance: pure machine learning on brain data, with a real clinical workflow as the target and an academic medical centre as a partner. The Cleveland Clinic relationship is the strongest signal in the entry, because an ICU monitoring deployment requires institutional trust that a seed-stage company does not usually get. But the funding picture is thin — $6M raised in 2024 with nothing since — and nothing from 2026 was found at all. The partnership and funding facts are now verified by Cleveland Clinic and trade coverage. Treat as a watch item: a Series A or an FDA submission would change it quickly, and either would be easy to spot.
