Sector: AI-native TechBio
Location: Somerville, MA
Summary: Gene Writing, one third smaller, and now largely a Regeneron-funded AATD company.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

A Flagship Pioneering company, founded 2018. Geoffrey von Maltzahn was co-founder and founding CEO, and is now Chairman. Michael Severino led the company from June 2022. Joseph Romanelli became President and CEO on 23 July 2026. Von Maltzahn also co-founded Generate, Cellarity, Lila Sciences and Quotient, so this entry sits inside the same Flagship cluster as several others in this file.

PLATFORM

Gene Writing: in vivo gene editing that writes sequence rather than only cutting it. The platform is positioned against both nuclease editing and viral gene therapy.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- TSRA-196 — lead in vivo gene-editing programme, for alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency. It has entered the clinic. FDA Fast Track and Orphan Drug designations came in February 2026.
- Regeneron collaboration on TSRA-196 became effective January 2026. Tessera runs the first-in-human trial; Regeneron leads subsequent global development and commercialisation. Note what that division means: Tessera has handed the asset's future to its partner.
- Sickle cell disease: pursuing a development candidate. $50M from the Gates Foundation supports this work.
- In vivo CAR-T work also continues.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Flagship-backed. Regeneron made aggregate payments of $150.0M in Q1 2026, covering an upfront payment and a purchase of Tessera preferred stock, with shared costs and profits thereafter. $50M came from the Gates Foundation for sickle cell. The Regeneron money is the reason the company still runs, and it arrived in the same quarter as the layoffs.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Cut 90 jobs from 8 March 2026, about 35% of staff, leaving roughly 160. One tracker put the starting headcount at 250 and the cut at 36%. No computational posting was enumerated. A company of 160 that has outsourced late-stage development to a partner will have a small dry-lab function.

ASSESSMENT

The pattern here is a platform company narrowing to one partnered asset. That is a survival structure, not a growth one, and the trend stays shrinking. Three leadership changes in four years is its own signal. For this profile the entry is mainly useful as a warning about Flagship platform companies: a strong scientific thesis and Flagship backing did not prevent a 35% cut. Do not pursue, but keep it visible as a comparison case for Cellarity and Generate.
