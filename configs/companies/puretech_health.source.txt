Summary: Hub-and-spoke venture builder; owns stakes rather than running one pipeline.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

A Boston-based, LSE-listed biotherapeutics company. It is not a conventional drug company. It originates programmes in-house, de-risks them clinically and technically at the hub, then scales each one through a separately funded "Founded Entity" backed mainly by outside capital. PureTech keeps the economics through equity, milestones and royalties. Daphne Zohar was CEO before leaving to run Seaport Therapeutics, which PureTech itself created.

PLATFORM

The model is the platform. Treat this entry as a portfolio rather than a technology: its scientific range is whatever its Founded Entities are working on. For this file that makes PureTech useful as a map of where new Boston companies will come from next, rather than as a single technical target.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Celea Therapeutics — holds the lead asset deupirfenidone (LYT-100) for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis. Reported to have raised $180M in July 2026, with PureTech holding a 35.4% fully diluted stake.
- Seaport Therapeutics — now listed on Nasdaq. Its own entry in this file covers it.
- Other Founded Entities continue; the roster changes as entities are spun out and deconsolidated.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Cash at year-end 2025 was reported at about $277M, and at 30 June 2026 at about $220M. Management guides to a runway through at least the end of 2028. Two reported items need checking against the primary filing before use. First, the Nasdaq ADS listing is reported to have ended in May 2026, leaving the London Main Market as the primary venue and ADSs quoted over the counter. Second, the Seaport stake is reported at roughly 42-43%, up from about 35%, which does not sit easily beside the deconsolidation accounting. Both come from secondary summaries. Verify in the H1 2026 half-year report.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Not enumerated this pass. A hub-and-spoke company hires a small central team and pushes scientific headcount into the Founded Entities. So the jobs this model creates mostly appear under other names, which is the practical reason to track it.

ASSESSMENT

The reason to keep this entry is forward visibility, not employment. PureTech is where several Boston companies in this file came from, and its Founded Entities are the next ones. Watching its pipeline tells you which Boston startups will be hiring in eighteen months, before they have a careers page. Seaport is the proof the model works. Note also that the listing and stake details are unresolved from outside, so do not quote its corporate structure without checking the half-year report.
