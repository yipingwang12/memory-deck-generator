Sector: Neurotech - noninvasive imaging
Location: Los Angeles, CA
Summary: Wearable near-infrared brain imaging helmet.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2016 in Los Angeles by Bryan Johnson, who funded it personally at first. Reports of the amount differ: one account gives $54M, and 2016-17 coverage gives a $100M commitment. So no card uses a figure. Ryan Field, who previously led the Kernel Flow team as CTO, is CEO. Kernel's own site lists Field as CEO and CTO. An earlier pass recorded Johnson as chairman. This pass did not confirm that.

PLATFORM

Kernel Flow: time-domain functional near-infrared spectroscopy (TD-fNIRS) that infers cortical blood flow and oxygenation from photon scattering. A 2022 paper in the Journal of Biomedical Optics describes Flow as keeping the performance of a research-grade TD-fNIRS system in a small modular device. A 2024 Scientific Reports study of 49 participants found high reliability of resting-state brain metrics; all its authors were Kernel employees. Flow2 combines TD-fNIRS with EEG. Its spec sheet labels it for research use only, with whole-cortex imaging at 3.76 Hz and a weight of 2.5 kg.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Clinical studies in treatment-resistant depression and healthy aging; reported ~90% accuracy predicting depression-treatment response before first dose.
- The FDA authorised a study using Kernel Flow to measure the effect of ketamine on the brain.

ASSESSMENT

Quiet in 2026. Depression prediction gives Shaohuan relevance; otherwise low priority. The asymmetry in this entry is worth stating plainly: the measurement technology is validated in a peer-reviewed journal and has an FDA-authorised study behind it, while the company itself has produced no public news for about two years. Judge the instrument and the business separately. As an employer this ranks near the bottom of the file. As a measurement platform that a research group might buy and use, it is credible.
