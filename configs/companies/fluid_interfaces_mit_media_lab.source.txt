HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Pattie Maes's research group at the MIT Media Lab. Its stated principle is that artificial intelligence should augment human cognition rather than substitute for it, which is the clearest articulation in this file of the thesis behind the whole human-AI integration section in 12.

PLATFORM

Human-computer interaction research on wearable systems that support memory, attention and critical thinking. The method is building and studying prototypes with human participants, so the discipline is HCI and experimental psychology rather than computational biology.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- MemPal, a wearable fact-checker, and NeuroSkill are named projects on memory, attention and critical thinking.
- AlterEgo originated here: Arnav Kapur was lead author of the 2018 AlterEgo paper in this group, and the IP transferred from MIT in April 2025. AlterEgo has its own entry in this file.
- The group is also the origin point for the argument in 12 that augmentation, not replacement, is the useful frame.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Academic and Media Lab consortium funded. The Media Lab model is corporate membership, so the group's work is shaped partly by member company interests.
