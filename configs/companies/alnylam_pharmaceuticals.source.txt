Sector: Established biopharma
Location: Cambridge, MA
Summary: RNAi pioneer, now commercially successful — which is rare in Boston right now.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Incorporated 14 June 2002 in Cambridge by a group of five: Nobel laureate Phillip Sharp (MIT), Paul Schimmel (Scripps), David Bartel (Whitehead Institute), Thomas Tuschl (Rockefeller) and Phillip Zamore (UMass Medical School). The catalyst was Sharp's 1999 Genes and Development paper on using double-stranded RNA to silence genes in mammalian cells. Founded specifically to turn RNA interference into a programmable drug platform.

PLATFORM

RNA interference therapeutics — sequence-programmable gene silencing. Six approved medicines to date, beginning with the world's first approved RNAi therapeutic in 2018.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- AMVUTTRA and ONPATTRO — the TTR franchise. Q2 2026 net product revenue $1,012M and $18M respectively, $1,030M combined.
- ALN-6400 — Phase 2 initiated in von Willebrand disease.
- Mivelsiran — Phase 2 initiated in Down syndrome-associated Alzheimer's disease.
- Additional programs in hypertension, haemophilia and hepatitis B.
- 2026 goals: 4 clinical readouts, 3 ongoing pivotal studies, 3 Phase 2 initiations, 3+ new IND filings.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Public, NASDAQ ALNY. Recorded its first ever quarter above $1B in product revenue in Q1 2026. Note the caution: 2026 TTR franchise guidance was LOWERED to $4.2-4.5B, attributed to a market mix shifting toward roughly 80% first-line and 20% second-line use. Launched an "Alnylam 2030" strategy.
