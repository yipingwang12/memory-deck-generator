HISTORY AND PEOPLE

A Cambridge-founded mRNA company now based in Boston. This file previously recorded Cambridge as its location; the company has said it is relocating operations into Lilly Gateway Labs, inside the Lilly Seaport Innovation Center. That makes it a tenant of another entry in this file, which is a second, quieter route into the Lilly site.

PLATFORM

An mRNA platform the company calls mRNA 2.0, with onco-selective design. Synthetic mRNA is delivered to T cells by lipid nanoparticle, so the CAR is built inside the patient rather than in a manufacturing facility. The company claims two consequences: no genomic integration risk, and minimised off-target effects. It also claims manufacturing cost could fall by as much as 100-fold. All three are company claims and none was independently checked.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- KR-402 — in vivo mRNA-encoded CAR-T. Indications are multiple sclerosis and B-cell malignancies, including acute lymphoblastic leukaemia, large B-cell lymphoma and chronic lymphocytic leukaemia.
- Funded through ARPA-H's EMBODY programme, which targets engineering immune cells inside the body.
- Sub-awardees: Stanford University School of Medicine, Dana-Farber Cancer Institute and The Jackson Laboratory, working on the mRNA-encoded CARs, manufacturing strategy and preclinical models.
- Listed to present its mRNA platform and T-cell-targeted LNPs at ASGCT 2026.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Awarded up to $48M from ARPA-H, announced 7 October 2025. The caution carried from the earlier row has weakened. The ARPA-H Investor Catalyst Hub in Cambridge did receive a termination notice on 30 January 2026, but ARPA-H subsequently called the closure reports false and said in March 2026 that the Boston hub is staying. That hub's entry in this file has been corrected from `gone` to `shrinking`. So the risk to this award is disruption rather than cancellation, and the award runs through the EMBODY programme rather than through the hub itself. Still confirm before treating the $48M as fully live. No private round was found.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Not enumerated this pass. The Dana-Farber sub-award is the useful detail for this profile: it puts a Boston academic computational group inside the programme, so there is a route in through Dana-Farber as well as through the company.

ASSESSMENT

Scientifically the most interesting of the promoted AI-native group, because in vivo CAR-T removes the manufacturing bottleneck that limits cell therapy. Two structural points make it worth tracking rather than pursuing. The money is almost entirely one government award whose administering hub has been terminated, and the company is small enough to be moving into shared space. Its new address is the strongest link: a Lilly Gateway Labs tenant sits beside Lilly's own 500-researcher site.

- KR-402 is an in vivo mRNA-encoded CAR-T programme
- Awarded up to $48M by ARPA-H under the EMBODY programme, October 2025
