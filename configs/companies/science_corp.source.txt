Sector: Neurotech - implant BCI
Location: Alameda, CA
Summary: Retinal implant restoring vision; first BCI-class product on a market.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2021 by Max Hodak, a Neuralink co-founder.

PLATFORM

PRIMA: a wireless photovoltaic retinal implant paired with glasses that project near-infrared light; acquired from France's Pixium Vision in April 2024 for about EUR 4M plus staff. Also a biohybrid program: stem-cell-derived neurons on a device that grow into the brain.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- PRIMA for geographic atrophy (late-stage dry macular degeneration): NEJM results; 80% of patients showed a clinically meaningful acuity improvement at 12 months.
- The implant is a 2 mm x 2 mm photovoltaic chip placed under the atrophic macula. Glasses project near-infrared light onto it.
- European commercial launch announced 22 July 2026. The CE mark came from DEKRA, a German notified body, under the EU Medical Device Regulation, and covers 30 European countries.
- Science calls PRIMA the first BCI device CE-marked for form vision restoration, meaning reading letters, numbers and words.
- Reimbursement is not yet secured. Country-by-country applications and clinical site activations are still in progress, so a CE mark alone does not let hospitals start implanting.
- The first commercial implant was expected in Germany shortly after launch; the actual date was not confirmed.
- US: Breakthrough and Humanitarian Use Device designations; FDA review still under way; possibly available 2027.
- Biohybrid neural interface: mouse proof of concept (2024 preprint).

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

$230M Series C (Mar 2026) at $1.5B; ~$490M raised in total.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Hardware, clinical, and neural-engineering roles; Bay Area. A European commercial launch also implies market-access, reimbursement and field-clinical hiring in Europe rather than in the US.

ASSESSMENT

The most commercially advanced BCI company, via an acquired device. It is now genuinely on sale, not approaching sale. Read the reimbursement caveat carefully, because it is the real gate: the CE mark permits sales, and without national reimbursement the addressable market stays small. The biohybrid program is still the most biology-heavy work in implant BCI and the better long-term fit for this profile. California.
