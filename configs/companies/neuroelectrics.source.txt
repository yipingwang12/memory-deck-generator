Sector: Neurotech - noninvasive neuromodulation
Location: Barcelona, Spain; Cambridge, MA office
Summary: At-home transcranial electrical stimulation, in pivotal trials for epilepsy.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded in Barcelona by Ana Maiques (CEO and co-founder) and Giulio Ruffini (co-founder and Chief Scientific Officer). The company is run from Barcelona and has its US base in Cambridge, Massachusetts. Its funding releases since 2021 describe it as based in Cambridge and Barcelona. An earlier pass put the Cambridge office at the Cambridge Innovation Center. This pass did not confirm that.

PLATFORM

Starstim: multichannel transcranial electrical stimulation (tES, including tDCS) combined with EEG. The company personalises it by modelling each patient's brain from MRI. Patients can use it at home under remote supervision.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Refractory focal epilepsy: the FDA granted Breakthrough Device Designation in June 2021. A pilot study in 20 adult and paediatric patients showed a median 44% reduction in seizure frequency.
- The pivotal trial is NCT04770337, randomised, double-blind and sham-controlled. The plan was about 190 patients with ten sessions over two weeks. The registry lists it as completed, with a primary completion date of 12 January 2026, and carries no posted outcomes.
- Alexander Rotenberg, Professor of Neurology at Boston Children's Hospital and Harvard Medical School, is co-principal investigator of the pivotal study.
- Earlier FDA-sanctioned IDE data: a 40% seizure-frequency reduction in 75% of patients at eight weeks, with no device-related adverse events.
- Major depressive disorder: an at-home telemedicine pilot in 35 adults with medication-resistant depression, reported in 2024, showed a 64% median reduction in depressive symptoms.
- A CURE Epilepsy review from August 2026 says the pivotal data were to support a PMA submission. No PMA filing and no approval were found in November 2026.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

$17.5M Series A (May 2021) led by Morningside Ventures, for the epilepsy pivotal trial and at-home depression treatment; ~$18.2M total disclosed. No later round was found. A company running a pivotal trial on about $18M is unusually thin, so check the balance sheet before you treat the US office as durable.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Mostly Barcelona. The Cambridge office is commercial and clinical, and it is the company's US base. A commercial office is not a research seat, so this entry is a market signal and not a target.

ASSESSMENT

A therapy company, not augmentation. Depression program gives Shaohuan relevance. The FDA decision is still the thing to check, and it has now slipped past its own target month without an announcement. Read that carefully: the trial completed in January 2026 and no result has been posted nine months later. Silence after a completed pivotal trial is more often a bad sign than a good one. Ana Maiques calls the programme the first FDA-sanctioned clinical trial of transcranial current stimulation in any indication, so the regulatory path itself is novel and may simply be slow.

KEY FACTS

- Barcelona-founded by Ana Maiques (CEO) and Giulio Ruffini (CSO)
- Device: Starstim transcranial electrical stimulation with EEG
- Lead indication: refractory focal epilepsy; FDA Breakthrough designation 2021
- Epilepsy pilot: median 44% seizure reduction; depression pilot: 64% median symptom reduction
- $17.5M Series A led by Morningside Ventures, 2021
- Pivotal trial co-PI Alexander Rotenberg, Boston Children's
