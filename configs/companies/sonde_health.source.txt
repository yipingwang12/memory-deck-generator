PLATFORM

Vocal biomarkers. The platform analyses subtle changes in a voice sample that correlate with physiology, so a short recording becomes a screening signal. The data asset is the substance of the company: over 1.2 million voice samples from more than 85,000 subjects across four continents. For a methods profile that is the interesting part — a large, labelled, multi-site signal dataset is exactly what a model-building role needs.
