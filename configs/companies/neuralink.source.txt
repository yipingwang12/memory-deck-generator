Sector: Neurotech - implant BCI
Location: Fremont, CA
Summary: The best-funded BCI company; ~20 implanted patients.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2016 by Elon Musk with a founding team of scientists and engineers. Max Hodak (later Science Corp) and Benjamin Rapoport (later Precision) were early members.

PLATFORM

N1 implant: 1,024 electrodes on ultrafine threads placed by a surgical robot, wireless.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Telepathy: computer control for paralysis. The patient count is not reliably knowable from outside. Sources give nine in January 2026, 21 in February 2026 and 26 in June 2026, and the last of those is relayed second-hand from a post on X. Treat ~20 as the defensible figure and do not card any exact number.
- Speech restoration: FDA Breakthrough designation May 2025. The programme is the VOICE trial, which decodes words from thought for people with severe speech impairment.
- GB PRIME study in Great Britain: seven patients enrolled as of May 2026.
- Blindsight: visual-cortex stimulation for blindness; first human trial planned 2026 with Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi. No implant has been confirmed. Reports disagree on the location, with one naming the UAE.
- A 2025 investor document put US regulatory approval for Telepathy at 2029 and a Blindsight launch at 2030, with a target of 20,000 patients by 2031.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

$650M Series E (Apr 2026) at ~$9B; ~$1.85B raised in total. Secondary-market talk far higher (unconfirmed).

TEAMS AND ROLES

Large engineering organisation; neural decoding ML, robotics, hardware. No posting was enumerated this pass.

ASSESSMENT

Leader on capital and patients, but it is in California, the work is signal decoding, and the culture is reported as intense. Not a fit for the family constraints. One point of general use: the company's own 2029 and 2030 dates are much later than its public tone suggests. Use those dates, not the announcements, when you judge how fast this field will actually produce approved products. They also set the pace for every other entry in the implant group.

KEY FACTS

- Founded 2016 by Elon Musk and team
- Implant: N1, 1,024 electrodes, placed by surgical robot
- Vision program named Blindsight
- Speech programme is the VOICE trial
- Max Hodak and Benjamin Rapoport were early members, and later founded Science Corp and Precision
