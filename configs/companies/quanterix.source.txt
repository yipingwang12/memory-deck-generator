Sector: Diagnostics and genomics tools
Location: Billerica, MA (plus Marlborough via Akoya)
Summary: Ultrasensitive blood biomarkers plus spatial proteomics; guidance cut in 2026.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Co-founded April 2007 by David R. Walt and Nicholas J. Naclerio. Walt co-invented the core Simoa technology, received the National Medal of Technology and Innovation at the White House in January 2025, and was awarded the Kabiller Prize in Nanoscience and Nanomedicine. Walt stepped down from the board effective November 2025. Everett Cunningham was appointed President and CEO effective 19 January 2026.

PLATFORM

Simoa — single-molecule array technology that detects and quantifies protein biomarkers in blood and other fluids at concentrations far below conventional immunoassay limits. Akoya adds spatial proteomics in tissue, giving a combined blood-plus-tissue biomarker ecosystem.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- LucentAD Complete — Alzheimer's blood test. Commercial coverage from Anthem Blue Cross and Blue Shield effective 1 July 2026.
- Neurofilament light chain and related neuro biomarkers.
- Selected as a co-investigator in the Michael J. Fox Foundation PD-BUILD program for Parkinson's.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Public, NASDAQ QTRX. Acquired Akoya Biosciences in an all-stock deal announced Jan 2025 and completed the same year. Q1 2026 revenue $36.4M (+20% YoY). Q2 2026 revenue $32.9M, up 34% as reported but DOWN 23% pro forma including pre-acquisition Akoya revenue. Full-year 2026 guidance cut to $142-148M from $169-174M, and the cash-flow breakeven target pushed from H2 2026 into 2027. Akoya integration completed in Q2 with $85M of annualised savings achieved and non-GAAP gross margin up 600 basis points.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Combined install base of approximately 2,300 instruments. Not separately verified for computational openings.

ASSESSMENT

Spatial biology is a direct hit on the Starfysh and RCTD experience, and the Alzheimer's blood-biomarker work is genuinely interesting. But the pro-forma revenue decline and the guidance cut say the combined company is shrinking, not compounding. Billerica and Marlborough are also car-dependent and fail the 03 filter outright.
