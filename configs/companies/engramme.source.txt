Sector: AI memory augmentation
Location: San Francisco (formerly Boston)
Summary: Large memory models that surface anything from your digital life, unprompted.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2025 by Gabriel Kreiman and Spandan Madan. Kreiman is a Harvard Medical School professor and neuroscientist who works at the AI-neuroscience interface. Kreiman closed his academic lab to found the company. Most reports give Kreiman as CEO. Madan, a former Harvard PhD student in computer science from Kreiman's lab, is CTO. Out of stealth March 2026. The company moved into a San Francisco office and grew from 2 to ~20 people.

PLATFORM

"Large Memory Models" modelled on how the brain encodes and retrieves memories. They connect to a user's email, documents and other digital records and recall relevant items automatically, without search or prompting. Three stated design goals: lifelong storage at petabyte scale, proactive retrieval, and associative recall. Tagline: "omniscient AI to augment human cognition". In 2026 the company opened its Memory API to a public beta, for app developers and product teams rather than consumers.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Memory API in public beta for developers.
- Consumer/professional memory product; no hardware.
- No independent benchmark of the models has been published.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

$3M pre-seed led by Mayfield. Bloomberg reported in April 2026 that the company was in talks to raise about $100M, and that prospective investors had discussed a valuation as high as $1B. Terms were not final. A search in October 2026 found no announcement that the round closed, so six months later the $3M pre-seed is still the only confirmed money.

TEAMS AND ROLES

~20 staff; ML research and engineering. Grew from 2 people to about 20 after the move.

ASSESSMENT

The purest human-memory augmentation company found, and needs no neuroscience hardware. It has left Boston, and Shaohuan has vetoed the Bay Area (02), so only a remote role would work. The $1B-on-$3M-pre-seed talk is a hype flag, and it is now a louder one: the round was reported as in-talks in April 2026 and has still not been confirmed as closed. Treat the valuation as press and not as fact. One search warning: do not confuse Engramme with Engram. Engram is a different San Francisco company. It left stealth in June 2026 with a reported $98M at about a $600M post-money valuation, and its founders come from Stanford, Berkeley and Cornell. The two work on adjacent problems with near-identical names, so always search this entry as "Engramme", with the final e.
