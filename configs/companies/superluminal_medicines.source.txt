Sector: AI-native TechBio
Location: Boston, MA
Summary: ML plus protein dynamics plus agentic cryo-EM, aimed at GPCRs.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Formed January 2022 by Cony D'Cruz and Ajay Yekkirala among others; launched publicly August 2023 with $33M in seed funding. The name was suggested by RA Capital's Nathaniel Brooks Horowitz and senior associate Soumya Ray for a then-secret project about compressing drug-discovery timelines. D'Cruz was previously EVP and Chief Business Officer at Schrodinger for eight years, and before that at Proteros and Evotec. Ajay Yekkirala is CSO, having joined at founding as Head of Discovery.

PLATFORM

The "Hyperloop" platform integrates structural biology, protein dynamics and machine learning to produce candidate-ready small molecules against membrane protein targets. Its "Agentic Cryo-EM" capability runs cryo-electron microscopy through AI agents at scale without human intervention, generating hundreds of empirical GPCR structures.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Lead: a best-in-class, selective, biased MC4R agonist for rare and genetic forms of obesity. Expected to enter the clinic by end-2026.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

$33M seed (2023), $120M Series A (Lilly-backed), $60M Series B (Sep 2026, oversubscribed). $213M total. Series B led by BVF Partners with Deep Track Capital and Perceptive Advisors as new investors, alongside RA Capital, Insight Partners, NVIDIA, Catalio, Eli Lilly, Cooley and Gaingels. Eli Lilly collaboration worth up to $1.3B in potential payments.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Transitioning from discovery platform to clinical-stage organisation, which is the phase at which companies typically add translational and computational headcount. Small; expect a flat organisation where a senior hire has real scope.

ASSESSMENT

Freshly funded, ML-central, and the investor list (NVIDIA, Lilly, RA Capital) is a strong signal. Structural biology and protein dynamics rather than genomics, so this is a genuine domain pivot — but the "builds models" story is unambiguous, which is precisely the keystone.
