Summary: Research and Early Development centre at Cambridge Crossing; informatics named in its own scope.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Established pharma, headquartered in Princeton, New Jersey. Chris Boerner became CEO on 1 November 2023 and Board Chair on 1 April 2024. BMS completed its acquisition of Celgene on 20 November 2019, a deal of about $74B. As a condition of that deal, the FTC required BMS to sell Otezla, and Amgen bought it for $13.4B. BMS completed its acquisition of Karuna Therapeutics on 18 March 2024, for about $14B. BMS completed its acquisition of 2seventy bio on 13 May 2025. The Cambridge building at 250 Water Street is purpose-built. Construction started in autumn 2019. The topping-off ceremony was in February 2021. Completion was planned for January 2023, with staff moving in over the following months. DivcoWest developed it. The BMS lease made 250 Water Street the fourth fully leased science and technology building at Cambridge Crossing. Job postings from mid-2026 place active roles at the site, so it is in use. No opening date and no current headcount were confirmed this pass.

PLATFORM

BMS calls the site a Research and Early Development centre. Its stated research areas are cancer resistance, neuroscience, and immune-related diseases. The site holds discovery biology, translational science and chemistry research. It also supports end-to-end drug discovery. That scope explicitly includes biotherapeutics, informatics and predictive sciences, and drug metabolism and pharmacokinetics. "Informatics and predictive sciences" named inside the site's own scope is the reason this entry rates fit 2 rather than fit 1. BMS is also a Nabla Bio generative-protein-design partner.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Broad portfolio; oncology, immunology and cardiovascular.
- Site-level focus: cancer resistance, neuroscience, immune-related diseases.
- Cobenfy came with Karuna. The FDA approved it for schizophrenia in adults on 26 September 2024. It is the first antipsychotic that acts on cholinergic receptors rather than dopamine receptors.
- The 2seventy bio deal gave BMS full control of Abecma, a BCMA-directed CAR-T therapy for multiple myeloma. 2seventy bio was spun out of bluebird bio in 2021.
- Breyanzi is BMS's CD19-directed CAR-T therapy for large B-cell lymphoma, approved in February 2021.
- Devens, Massachusetts: the FDA approved BMS's cell therapy manufacturing plant there in June 2023. It is BMS's third commercial CAR-T manufacturing facility in the US, on an 89-acre campus, and it makes Breyanzi.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Corporate. BMS leased space at 250 Water Street from DivcoWest to consolidate operations. The lease grew in two steps. BMS first leased about 360,000 sq ft in 2020. In 2021 it took the rest of the building, about 113,000 sq ft more, and the March 2022 BMS release gives about 480,000 sq ft. The two figures earlier in this file were therefore both right, at different dates. An earlier $280M Massachusetts expansion was reported as creating up to 350 jobs.

TEAMS AND ROLES

The 2022 release said more than 550 employees would move in, from Kendall Square and Alewife. Current headcount was not enumerated this pass. Cancer immunology translational research roles at Senior Scientist, Principal Scientist and Scientific Associate Director level were posted for the site between May and June 2026, which shows the hiring level is senior.

ASSESSMENT

Growing footprint in a market where most large tenants are shrinking theirs. Same buying-not-building pattern as Takeda and AstraZeneca on the AI side. The useful detail is that informatics and predictive sciences sit inside the site's own remit, so a computational seat here is in the research organisation and not in a separate IT function.
