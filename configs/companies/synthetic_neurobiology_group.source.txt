HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Ed Boyden's group at MIT, about 60 people. Boyden is the most connected individual in this dossier: he co-founded Mindspan, Cognito Therapeutics and Expansion Technologies, advises E11 Bio, and co-founded Elemind. Six entries in this file trace to this one group, which is why it matters more as a network node than as a single employer.

PLATFORM

Tool building. The group invents measurement and control methods and gives them away, and the companies follow afterwards. Optogenetics came from here, as did expansion microscopy. The relevant pattern for this profile is that several of these tools produce sequencing or spatial data, which is where the computational need sits.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- 1000ExM — expansion microscopy pushed to a thousandfold volumetric expansion, 2026.
- ExSeq — in situ transcriptomics, developed with George Church. This is the entry's closest point of contact with the CV: spatial transcriptomics generated by a Boyden method.
- In situ protein sequencing.
- Whole-brain voltage imaging, Nature Methods, August 2026.
- Optogenetics, the group's best-known earlier contribution.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Academic, philanthropic and grant funded. Open Philanthropy has given an unrestricted gift to the MIT lab for brain-mapping work, covering materials, equipment and salaries for up to nine postdoctoral researchers, staff scientists and graduate students over two years. That gift went to the lab, not to Mindspan.

TEAMS AND ROLES

ASSESSMENT

Approach this as the hub of a network rather than as a job. ExSeq is the specific hook: in situ transcriptomics developed here is the same class of data as the Jia Liu group's electro-sequencing, and both are in Cambridge. The group's output is methods that other people's companies commercialise, so knowing it well gives early sight of several future employers — Mindspan is the current example. The constraint is that no staff posting has ever been observed across two research passes, so the realistic routes are the Open Philanthropy-funded staff scientist positions or Mindspan itself.
