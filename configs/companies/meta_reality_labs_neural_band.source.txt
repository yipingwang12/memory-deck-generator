Sector: Neurotech - neuromuscular interface
Location: Redmond, WA / New York
Summary: Wrist EMG band shipping with Meta's display glasses; the largest consumer neural interface.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Came from CTRL-labs (founded by Thomas Reardon and Patrick Kaifosh), acquired by Facebook in 2019. Reardon leads neuromotor interfaces at Reality Labs.

PLATFORM

Surface electromyography at the wrist decodes intended finger movements and handwriting into commands. Nature 2025 paper showed generic models that work across users without calibration.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Meta Neural Band shipped 30 Sep 2025, bundled with Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses ($799 at the Connect launch on 17 September 2025).
- In May 2026 Meta opened Ray-Ban Display to third-party developers by two routes: a native mobile SDK and a web-apps path. Web apps can be distributed by URL without an app store.
- Developers also got Neural Band gesture control, which uses surface EMG to read small finger and hand movements.
- One account states that the first toolkit version cannot reach the Neural Band EMG sensors directly. That contradicts the gesture-control reports, so assume gestures are exposed and raw sEMG is not.
- No unit sales have been published for either the glasses or the band.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Corporate.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Large-scale biosignal ML: data collection from thousands of users, cross-user generalisation, on-device models. The 2026 developer opening suggests platform and developer-tooling work alongside the research roles.

ASSESSMENT

Proof that a neural-signal interface for healthy users can ship at scale, and the most data-heavy ML in consumer neurotech. Big-tech pay; not Boston. The 2026 developer opening matters for a different reason than hiring: once third parties build on the band, Meta gains usage data from applications it did not design, and that is the largest neural-signal dataset anyone in this file will hold. Note also that no independent SDK assessment exists yet, so how much of the band the platform really exposes is still unclear.
