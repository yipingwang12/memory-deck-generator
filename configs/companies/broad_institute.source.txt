Sector: Academic and non-profit
Location: Kendall Square, Cambridge, MA
Summary: The centre of gravity for this entire profile.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2003, co-founded by Harvard and MIT, explicitly designed to translate academic findings rather than only publish them. Eric Lander was founding director. Its alumni network seeded a large share of the companies in this file — Foundation Medicine, Beam, Editas and others trace directly to Broad faculty.

PLATFORM

Organised into platforms and programs rather than departments. Relevant units include the Genomics Platform, the Data Sciences Platform, the Klarman Cell Observatory (single-cell and cell circuits; founded and led by Aviv Regev until she left for Genentech in 2020, with Ramnik Xavier now associated with the team), and the Stanley Center for Psychiatric Research.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Klarman Cell Observatory — began 2012 to define cellular circuits in mammalian cells; second phase from 2017 broadened to how cells work together in tissues and how human genetic variants perturb pathways to cause disease.
- Broad Scalable Services, long-read sequencing applications, and the Talkowski lab at MGH and Broad, which works on structural variant detection.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Philanthropically and grant funded. Massachusetts ranks second nationally for NIH funding at 7,037 awards worth $4.34B.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Named among the top employers hiring computational biologists in Cambridge. Openings seen this pass include Computational Scientist in Broad Scalable Services, Computational Scientist I on applications of long-read sequencing, an Early Career Researchers Computational Associate I program with Broad-wide openings, and a Computational Scientist in the Talkowski Laboratory at MGH's Center for Genomic Medicine analysing sequencing data to identify structural variants. Requirements cited include scientific and numerical programming in Python or R, knowledge of machine learning methods, and experience with next-generation sequencing pipelines — which is close to a description of the current job. 01's bands: Computational Scientist I around $92,700 median, Senior Computational Scientist around $133,000, Senior Software Engineer around $147,000; Research Scientist I reported at around $113,000 median total pay.

ASSESSMENT

The Talkowski structural-variant role is the single most on-target posting found this pass. Compensation is the constraint, not fit — see 04, where one academic salary reaches West Medford at best.
