Sector: AI-native TechBio
Location: Boston, MA
Summary: Genetic circuit design sold as a bundle of components, CAD software and generative models.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2017. The launch team included Alec Nielsen, Chris Voigt of MIT, Doug Densmore and Raja Srinivas. The company was built on Cello, an academic circuit-design tool, and the founding idea was that a genetic circuit can be designed the way an electronic circuit is. The seed round was $4.7M led by Andreessen Horowitz, with DCVC, Pillar and AME Cloud Ventures.

PLATFORM

Asimov sells a bundle, not a single product. It combines libraries of proprietary genetic components, computer-aided design software, and large generative AI models, and licenses the biology and the software together to biotech companies. The applied target is cell-line and genetic-circuit design for biologics manufacturing, so the customer is a manufacturing organisation rather than a drug discovery group.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- RNA Edge — launched March 2026. An integrated AI, synthetic biology and laboratory platform for optimising RNA therapeutics.
- Chrysalis — announced 23 June 2026. A proprietary genome integration tool, licensable on its own or used through Asimov's cell line development services.
- LV Edge — a lentiviral packaging cell line. A licensing agreement with AGC Biologics followed in January 2026.
- Lonza — a strategic collaboration on lentiviral vector manufacturing announced 2 October 2026.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Over $200M raised. The January 2023 round was $200M and included a $175M Series B led by CPP Investments. Other investors include a16z, Horizons Ventures and Fidelity. No 2026 round was found, and none is needed on that base.

TEAMS AND ROLES

A Senior Scientist posting in biologics cell line development was open at the time of this pass, so the company hires at senior scientist level in Boston. The generative-model half of the platform implies ML roles, but no such posting was confirmed.

ASSESSMENT

The most commercially coherent company in the promoted AI-native group. It ships products rather than promises: three launches in 2026 and two manufacturing partners, AGC Biologics and Lonza. The fit is real but indirect — generative models applied to genetic circuits and cell lines, not to genomics — so the methods transfer and the domain story does not. Its strength for this file is stability: a 2023 Series B of $175M, revenue-bearing partnerships, and no distress signals in a market full of them.
