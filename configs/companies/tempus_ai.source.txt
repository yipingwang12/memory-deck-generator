Summary: Precision oncology data and AI; now the defendant on its own core liquid-biopsy products.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

A Chicago company built on the idea that clinical and molecular data from cancer care is itself the product. It sequences tumours, structures the resulting clinical data, and sells both diagnostics and data access. It appears twice elsewhere in this file: it is building a MYB poison-exon companion diagnostic with Remix Therapeutics, and it is the counterparty in the Guardant Health litigation described below.

PLATFORM

Sequencing plus a structured clinical-data layer plus AI models over the top. The xF and xM liquid biopsy assays are the core products. The data-plus-model combination is what makes the fit real for a genomics profile, even though the company is not in Boston.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- xF and xM liquid biopsy products are the commercial core.
- A pending acquisition of Personalis for about $1.5B, which would add another genomics company to the group.
- Companion diagnostic development with Remix Therapeutics on a MYB poison exon.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Public, NASDAQ TEM. A Q2 2026 summary reports GAAP profitability achieved, against about $1.2B of total convertible debt and the pending $1.5B Personalis acquisition. Those come from a secondary summary, so confirm against the filings; the FY2025 10-K is on EDGAR.

TEAMS AND ROLES

The Boston footprint is a small co-working office, so roles here are remote or Chicago-based. That has not changed. Treat this as a remote-or-relocate entry rather than a local one.

ASSESSMENT

The litigation framing in this file was backwards and needs correcting. The old row said Tempus was in litigation against Guardant over whether AI-enabled diagnostic patents claim eligible subject matter. What actually happened runs both ways. On 21 January 2026 the court granted Guardant's motion to dismiss Tempus's suit, and final judgment was entered for Guardant by stipulation with no damages; Law360 reported the dismissal on patent-eligibility grounds under Alice. Then in August 2026 a Delaware judge denied Guardant's dismissal bids in the other direction, so Tempus must face Guardant's claims that it infringes patents covering cell-free DNA tests. Two Guardant cases are live in Delaware, 1:24-cv-00687 and 1:25-cv-01013. So Tempus lost as plaintiff and is now defending its core xF and xM revenue. The January and August rulings are not fully reconciled across the sources found. That is a material risk the old row did not convey, and it sits on top of $1.2B of convertible debt and a $1.5B acquisition.
