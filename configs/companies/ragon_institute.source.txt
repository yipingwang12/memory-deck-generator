Sector: Academic and non-profit
Location: 600 Main St, Kendall Square, Cambridge
Summary: New building with dedicated computational space; single-cell immunology.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

A joint institute of Mass General Brigham, MIT and Harvard. Founding Director Bruce D. Walker, Professor of Medicine at Harvard Medical School, Professor of Practice at MIT and an HHMI Investigator, whose own research is on cellular immune responses in chronic viral infection, particularly HIV. Associate Director and Scientific Director is Facundo Batista.

PLATFORM

Immunology, with heavy single-cell and systems methods — the same territory as the Izar and Shalek lineages. The stated mission is to harness the immune system to prevent and cure human disease. The institute has three programs: Immune Engineering, Patient Focused Immunology, and Immunophysiology and Disease. Centralized Research Services support all three. These cores include flow cytometry, microscopy, biostatistics and a Biosafety Level 3 core. A biostatistics core inside the institute matters for a dry-lab seat.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- HIV immunology and vaccine development; broader infectious disease immunology.
- About 28 labs are listed, so the institute is a federation of labs rather than one group.
- FRESH and ITEACH programs in South Africa; a role in COVID-19 vaccine development.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Endowed and grant funded. The Phillip T. and Susan M. Ragon Foundation gave the founding money in 2009. The pledge was $100M, paid as $10M a year over ten years, and split between Harvard, MGH and MIT. A second gift of $200M endowed the institute in 2019. MGH called it the largest gift in the hospital's history. Ursula Brunner gave $5M in 2019 for the FRESH program. Phillip Ragon is an InterSystems founder and argued that flexible money, not grant money, changes how research is done. The endowment is the point for this file: it does not depend on the NIH cycle that 02 records as closing the U-M search.

TEAMS AND ROLES

The new headquarters at 600 Main Street opened in 2024: 323,000 gross square feet, LEED Gold, split roughly 50/50 wet and dry lab, with multiple spaces specifically for computational biologists, AI and machine learning. Three floors of BSL1/BSL2+ labs, one floor of BSL3, and a vivarium. Fellows receive a start-up package with salary, research support and dedicated lab and office space.

ASSESSMENT

A 50/50 wet-dry split with named computational space is an unusually explicit institutional commitment to dry-lab science. In Kendall Square, which is where the commute question is easiest.
