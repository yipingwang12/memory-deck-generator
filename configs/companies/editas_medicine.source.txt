Sector: Genomic medicine
Location: Cambridge, MA
Summary: Rebuilt as an in vivo editing company after dropping its clinical asset.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

One of the original CRISPR companies, founded on Broad Institute science. That lineage still matters: the Broad, Beam and Editas all trace to overlapping founders, and all three appear in this file.

PLATFORM

In vivo gene editing with lipid nanoparticle delivery. The scientific core is editing haematopoietic stem cells inside the patient. The company targets the HBG1 and HBG2 promoter sites with its AsCas12a enzyme. The strategic point is what this avoids: an in vivo edit needs no transplant, and therefore no myeloablative conditioning. Conditioning is the main reason approved ex vivo therapies such as Casgevy are hard to deliver at scale, so removing it is the whole commercial thesis.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- EDIT-401 — lead in vivo development candidate. Preclinically it showed roughly 90% or greater mean reduction in LDL-C, Lp(a) and ApoB. CTN submission was on track.
- In vivo HSC programmes targeting HBG1 and HBG2 remain preclinical.
- reni-cel — discontinued in December 2024. The ex vivo sickle cell and beta-thalassaemia candidate was dropped after the company failed to find a commercial partner.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Public, NASDAQ EDIT. Cash, equivalents and investments were $123.6M at 31 March 2026, which the company expected to fund operations into Q3 2027. One aggregator reports a May 2026 raise of about $117M net and a runway into the second half of 2028; that could not be corroborated against a filing and conflicts with the Q1 guidance, so treat it as unverified and check the latest 10-Q.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Not enumerated this pass. The 2024 restructuring cut 65% of staff, about 180 jobs over six months, including Chief Medical Officer Baisong Mei. What remains is a preclinical in vivo organisation, so the hiring that exists will be delivery, editing and translational, with a small computational function.

ASSESSMENT

This is a company that has already had its crisis and chosen a direction. The pivot is scientifically sound — conditioning-free in vivo editing is the right target — but everything is preclinical, and about $124M against a Q3 2027 runway is tight for that stage. Note the contrast with CRISPR Therapeutics in this file: the same founding era, the same field, and one has approved-product revenue while this one dropped its clinical asset for want of a partner. Keep it visible for the science and treat the balance sheet as the binding constraint.

KEY FACTS

- Pivoted from ex vivo to in vivo gene editing
- Lead in vivo candidate EDIT-401
- Edits HSCs in vivo using AsCas12a at the HBG1 and HBG2 promoters
- In vivo editing needs no myeloablative conditioning
- Discontinued reni-cel in December 2024 after failing to find a partner
