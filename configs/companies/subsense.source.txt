Sector: Neurotech - nanoparticle BCI
Location: California (Palo Alto, San Francisco and Covina all reported)
Summary: Nanoparticles by nasal spray, read through a headset. Mice only, and years out.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

A nanoparticle BCI company co-founded around 2021-22 by Tetiana Aleksandrova (co-founder and CEO) and, by one account, Artem Sokolov, founder of Golden Falcon Capital. Sources give the headquarters variously as Palo Alto, San Francisco and Covina, California. It pursues the most speculative approach in this file: deliver the interface as particles rather than as hardware, so no surgery is needed and no device enters the skull.

PLATFORM

NanoBCI. Engineered biocompatible nanoparticles are delivered by nasal spray, cross the blood-brain barrier and reach the brain through the olfactory bulb. Magnets then steer them to their targets. A wearable headset with magnetic coils reads and stimulates them. The stimulation programme uses magnetoelectric nanoparticles that respond to an applied magnetic field. The appeal is obvious and so is the difficulty: particle distribution, clearance, targeting and long-term safety are all unsolved, and none of them has a published answer.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Stated clinical targets are Parkinson's disease and epilepsy. A separate sensing programme aims at communication for people with ALS.
- Mouse proof-of-concept studies began in late 2025 and were expected to finish by late 2026, aiming to show neural reading plus symptom relief.
- Human pilots are projected for 2027. One report gives a 2027-2029 window.
- No peer-reviewed result for the core claim has been published. Fortune characterised the technology as years from availability.
- BrainGate pioneer John Donoghue advises the company on its scientific path.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

$27M in total: a $17M seed in 2025 plus a $10M extension from Golden Falcon Capital in December 2025. In September 2026 a company press release announced that Ray Kurzweil joined as a product and vision advisor, and Fortune reported it. One outlet added an editor's note disputing the claim, but the press release is primary.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Not enumerated. At mouse-study stage the work is nanoparticle chemistry, in vivo biology and signal physics. There is no large dataset yet, so there is no methods role of the kind this profile wants.

ASSESSMENT

Keep the hype flag the previous row carried, and strengthen it. The capital has grown to $27M and a famous advisor has attached his name, while the science remains at mouse proof-of-concept with human work no earlier than 2027. The pattern — notable advisor, striking mechanism, preclinical reality — is exactly what to discount. The entry is useful only as a marker of how much money noninvasive BCI concepts can raise on very little evidence. Not a target.

KEY FACTS

- NanoBCI delivers nanoparticles by nasal spray
- Particles are read and stimulated by a magnetic-coil headset
- Targets Parkinson's disease and epilepsy
- $17M seed, then a $10M extension from Golden Falcon Capital, 2025
- Advisors Ray Kurzweil and John Donoghue
