Sector: Neurotech - CNS drugs
Location: Framingham, MA
Summary: $106M launch by the Sage Therapeutics team; four in-licensed CNS assets near Phase 2.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Launched on 21 April 2026 from Framingham, Massachusetts. Jeff Jonas is CEO and Al Robichaud is President and Head of R&D. Both came from Sage Therapeutics, where they developed several CNS treatments including the first FDA-approved therapy for postpartum depression. That lineage is the most important fact in the entry, and it creates a direct link to Seaport Therapeutics elsewhere in this file: Seaport's lead asset is an oral prodrug of allopregnanolone, the same neurosteroid class that the Sage team brought to approval. Two Boston-area companies are now pursuing that chemistry, built by overlapping people.

PLATFORM

Not a platform company. The strategy is in-licensing clinical-stage CNS assets and running them through mid-stage trials, which is a development organisation rather than a discovery one. The four programmes were in-licensed from Eisai and Jiangsu Hansoh.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Four programmes targeting schizophrenia, tinnitus, focal epilepsy and reversible encephalopathies.
- Sources differ on stage: one says all four are at or near Phase 2, another that they are already in clinical testing.
- The stated spending priority is completing Phase 2 trials for the two lead candidates, in schizophrenia and tinnitus.
- Phase 2 claims are company-stated and were not independently confirmed this pass.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

$106M across a seed and a Series A, announced at launch. Cure Ventures led the seed and co-leads the Series A with The Column Group and AN Venture Partners. The split between rounds was not disclosed.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Not enumerated. A development-stage company with four in-licensed assets hires clinical operations, regulatory and biometrics people, not discovery scientists. The computational content would be clinical biostatistics.
