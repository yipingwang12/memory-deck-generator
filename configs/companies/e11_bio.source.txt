Sector: Neurotech - connectomics (nonprofit)
Location: Alameda, CA
Summary: Protein-barcode connectomics; a Boyden-alumni Focused Research Organization.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2021 as a Focused Research Organization under Convergent Research. CEO Andrew Payne (Boyden lab PhD); COO Jun Axup. Scientific advisors Ed Boyden, George Church and Stephen Smith.

PLATFORM

PRISM (Protein-barcode Reconstruction via Iterative Staining with Molecular annotations): gives each neuron a unique protein barcode, then combines expansion microscopy and ML so reconstructions self-correct. Claims ~100x cost reduction toward whole-brain connectomes.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- PRISM was announced in December 2024 as the first step toward cheaper, more accessible connectomics.
- A preprint released 1 October 2025 describes self-correcting neuron tracing using simple light microscopes, based on a mouse hippocampus pilot. Andrew Payne is among the authors.
- The hippocampal CA3 dataset is public on the AWS Open Data registry as "E11bio PRISM". It holds light microscopy, segmentations, visualisation files and annotations. Neurons were transduced with an AAV library encoding diverse protein fragments that act as combinatorial barcodes.
- Constructs are on Addgene, so the toolbox is reusable by outside labs.
- Roadmap toward whole mouse brain, then human scale.

ASSESSMENT

Same ecosystem as Mindspan, and ML-central, but Bay Area. FROs run on fixed terms, so job permanence is structurally limited. The open-data posture is the distinguishing feature: the dataset is on AWS, the constructs are on Addgene and the methods are in a preprint.
