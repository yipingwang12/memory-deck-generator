Sector: Genomic medicine
Location: Cambridge, MA
Summary: RNA editing that missed its potency threshold and is rebuilding around new delivery.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

An RNA editing company. The approach uses the cell's own ADAR enzymes, directed by an oligonucleotide, to make a single-base edit in RNA rather than in DNA. The appeal is that nothing in the genome is permanently changed.

PLATFORM

ADAR-mediated RNA editing delivered by oligonucleotide. The entry's history is a lesson in how delivery, not editing, decides these programmes.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- KRRO-110 — for alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency. The REWRITE trial did produce functional M-AAT protein in patients, so the editing mechanism worked in humans.
- But peak levels reached only about 2 micromolar against the 11 micromolar protective threshold the company cited. That is the reason the programme stopped: insufficient potency, not a safety failure.
- Safety was in fact favourable: no serious adverse events, and only mild infusion-related reactions that resolved quickly.
- The company attributed the shortfall to differences in the pharmacokinetics of the delivery components between healthy volunteers and AATD patients. That is a delivery problem, not an editing problem.
- Korro moved to a GalNAc-conjugated version of the AATD candidate, targeting a development candidate nomination in H1 2026.
- KRRO-121 — nominated for hyperammonemia, with clinic entry guided for H2 2026 and 2027.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Public. FY2024 guidance put the cash runway into the second half of 2026, which is older guidance and has probably changed. One headline reports a 34% workforce reduction and a sharp share price fall, but the body text was not retrievable this pass, so the date and detail of that cut are unconfirmed. Treat the balance sheet as the open question.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Not enumerated this pass, and probably reduced. A company that has narrowed to a re-engineered delivery candidate hires chemistry and delivery expertise ahead of computational biology.

ASSESSMENT

Honest failure, carefully diagnosed, which is more informative than most entries here. The editing worked in humans and the safety was clean; the delivery did not reach the therapeutic threshold, and the company said so with a specific mechanism. That is a scientifically credible team in a weak financial position. Do not pursue while the runway and the reported 34% cut are unresolved, but the GalNAc nomination in H1 2026 is the event to watch. Note the parallel with Wave in this file: both are Cambridge oligonucleotide companies whose science produced a human first and whose commercial path then narrowed.

KEY FACTS

- RNA editing using the cell's own ADAR enzymes
- KRRO-110 targeted alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency in the REWRITE trial
- Peak M-AAT reached ~2 micromolar against an 11 micromolar protective threshold
- Moved to a GalNAc-conjugated candidate after the potency shortfall
- KRRO-121 targets hyperammonemia
