HISTORY AND PEOPLE

PureTech Health assembled the company and launched it in spring 2024 with $100M. Daphne Zohar is the CEO. She was previously CEO of PureTech, and she co-founded Karuna, the company behind Cobenfy, the schizophrenia drug now owned by Bristol Myers Squibb. Steven Paul, Karuna's former CEO, chairs the board. PureTech has its own entry in this file, so read the two together: Seaport is PureTech's largest success to date and its deconsolidation changed PureTech's accounts.

PLATFORM

Glyph, a lymphatic-targeting prodrug technology. The drug is absorbed through the lymphatic system rather than the portal vein, which raises oral bioavailability and reduces first-pass metabolism in the liver. The point is to make an oral drug out of a molecule that otherwise needs infusion. SPT-300 is a prodrug of allopregnanolone, a natural neurosteroid whose synthetic form is marketed as Zulresso for postpartum depression and must be infused.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- SPT-300 (GlyphAllo) — lead asset, for depression. $121M of the IPO proceeds is earmarked for it.
- SPT-320 (GlyphAgo) — generalised anxiety disorder.
- SPT-348 (Glyph2BLSD) — under evaluation in multiple neuropsychiatric disorders.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

About $325M raised privately: $100M at launch in spring 2024 and a $225M Series B months later. The company then listed on Nasdaq in April 2026 as SPTX, selling more than 14 million shares at $18 and raising $254.9M. That beat the roughly $180M it had been expected to raise, and made it the first neurology-focused biotech to go public in 2026. Reported IPO valuation differs by source: $955M on one tracker and about $733M on another.

TEAMS AND ROLES

58 employees at the time of the IPO. No computational posting was enumerated. A company of that size with three clinical-stage assets runs clinical and translational work, not a discovery informatics group. Expect the dry-lab content here to be clinical data and pharmacometrics rather than genomics.
