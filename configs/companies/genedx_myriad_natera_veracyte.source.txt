HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Four clinical genomics and diagnostics companies, grouped as one row in the original file. They are the commercial counterparts to the clinical variant-interpretation work in the current CV. The row previously said "verify Boston presence individually"; that verification was done this pass and the answer is negative for the three that could be checked.

PLATFORM

Clinical sequencing and variant interpretation at volume. GeneDx runs rare-disease exome and genome testing. Natera covers oncology, women's health and organ health. Veracyte does cancer diagnostics from tissue and cytology. Myriad covers hereditary cancer and other inherited risk. The computational discipline is variant classification against clinical evidence, which is the closest commercial analogue to the DGG work anywhere in this file.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- GeneDx Q2 2026: total revenue $114.4M, up 11% year on year. Exome and genome revenue $100.3M, up 17%. Exome and genome volume 30,785 results, up 32%.
- GeneDx reaffirmed full-year 2026 guidance of $475-490M, with at least 30% exome and genome volume growth and at least 20% revenue growth. Q3 guidance was $122-124M.
- GeneDx margins: 70% adjusted gross margin, 68% GAAP. GAAP net loss $17.7M against adjusted net income of $0.4M.
- CEO Katherine Stueland has said demand for rare-disease testing has never been higher, and that improving reimbursement is the company's largest opportunity. That is the central economic problem of clinical genomics stated plainly.
- Natera's CLIA laboratories are in Austin TX, San Carlos CA and Boulder CO, per its 10-Q for the period ending 30 June 2026.
- Veracyte's laboratories are in South San Francisco CA, San Diego CA and Austin TX, with a facility in Haifa, Israel.
- Myriad Genetics locations could not be confirmed this pass.
