Sector: Neurotech - NeuroAI diagnostics
Location: Tel Aviv; US and Asia offices
Summary: A 6B-parameter foundation model on EEG; the largest ML-on-neural-data company found.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Came out of stealth on 15 July 2026 after about six years of work. Hagai Lalazar, a computational neuroscientist, is CEO. Gidi Littwin is CTO; he co-founded RealFace, which Apple acquired for Face ID. That pairing — a computational neuroscientist with someone who has shipped a biometric model at consumer scale — is the reason this entry reads differently from the other EEG companies in this file. Note that sources spell the CEO's surname both Lalazar and Lalazer, and one outlet calls the company Hemisphere.

PLATFORM

Descartes, a NeuroAI foundation model with 6 billion parameters. It was trained on a proprietary dataset of more than 250,000 hours of multimodal EEG and behavioural recordings from over 100,000 participants. The deployment side is deliberately light: a dry EEG headset and a session of about 15 minutes run from a tablet or phone. The product claim is to turn a noninvasive brain recording into clinical metrics, in the way a blood panel turns a sample into numbers.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Stated early clinical targets: PTSD, mild traumatic brain injury, depression, anxiety, schizophrenia and Alzheimer's disease.
- The company says it is working toward regulatory approval. No clearance was found.
- Stated uses of the funding: expanding deployments with healthcare, pharmaceutical and government partners, growing the brain-data network, and growing US operations.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

$52M in total, spanning pre-seed, seed and Series A, announced at launch. Named investors include Hanaco Ventures, OneMind Awareness Capital, Protocol Labs, L Catterton, Arkin Capital, OurCrowd and Artofin. 112 staff.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Remote and US roles were not verified. The company states that part of the raise funds growing US operations, which is the only hiring signal available and is not a posting. A 6-billion-parameter model trained on a proprietary dataset needs a substantial ML organisation, so the roles exist somewhere; where is the open question.

ASSESSMENT

On technical shape this is the best match in the entire neurotech group: a foundation model trained on a large proprietary biological dataset, which is precisely the build-methods-on-biological-data profile this dossier serves. The psychiatric target list also makes it directly relevant to Shaohuan. Two cautions. Every headline figure — the parameter count, the 250,000 hours, the 100,000 participants — comes from the company's own launch announcement, and no independent validation of model accuracy was found. And the centre of gravity is Tel Aviv, with US operations only now being built, so a US role may not yet exist in a meaningful form. Watch for the first independent validation study and for US postings; both would move this entry from interesting to actionable.

- Trained on over 250,000 hours of EEG and behavioural data from 100,000+ people
