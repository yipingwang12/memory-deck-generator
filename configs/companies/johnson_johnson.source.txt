HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Johnson & Johnson is headquartered in New Brunswick, New Jersey. Joaquin Duato became CEO in January 2022 and Chairman in January 2023. In September 2023 J&J renamed its pharmaceutical business, Janssen, as Johnson & Johnson Innovative Medicine; the device business is Johnson & Johnson MedTech. The renaming followed the 2023 separation of the consumer health business as Kenvue. Tom Cavanaugh became head of Innovative Medicine on 1 September 2026, succeeding Jennifer Taubert. Janssen is also the counterparty on Remix Therapeutics' 2022 deal.

PLATFORM

In Cambridge, Johnson & Johnson Innovation runs JLABS @ LabCentral in Kendall Square, the first East Coast JLABS site. It is an incubator for start-ups, not a J&J research group. No Cambridge-specific J&J research unit or computational group was identified this pass.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Momenta Pharmaceuticals, a Cambridge company: J&J completed its acquisition on 1 October 2020, for about $6.5B. It gave J&J full global rights to nipocalimab.
- Nipocalimab, branded Imaavy, was approved by the FDA in April 2025 for generalised myasthenia gravis.
- Abiomed, of Danvers, Massachusetts: J&J completed its acquisition on 22 December 2022, at an enterprise value of about $16.6B. Abiomed makes Impella heart pumps and runs as a standalone business within J&J MedTech.
- Q2 2026 Innovative Medicine sales were $16.4B, up 6.8% operationally, against an approximately 760 basis point headwind from STELARA losing exclusivity.
- Full-year 2026 guidance was raised to $11.60-$11.75 adjusted EPS on $100.8-$101.4B of revenue, with Innovative Medicine leading growth.
- Regulatory applications were filed for icotrokinra, branded Icotyde, in moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis, with approval considered likely during 2026.

ASSESSMENT

The company is growing and its Innovative Medicine unit is leading that growth, so this is not a distressed employer. Its Boston presence is real but indirect: it bought two Massachusetts companies and sponsors the start-up ecosystem through JLABS. For this profile, the Abiomed and Momenta lines are the useful leads, and the corporate facts are now verified enough to card.
