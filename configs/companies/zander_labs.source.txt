Sector: Neurotech - passive BCI
Location: Cottbus, Germany and Delft, Netherlands
Summary: Passive BCI on a EUR 30M German state contract; machines adapt to the user, not the reverse.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

A company that describes itself as German-Dutch; sources place it in Cottbus, Germany and in Delft, Netherlands. Professor Thorsten Zander leads the science and Gerard de Jager is CEO and chairs the steering board.

PLATFORM

Passive brain-computer interfaces. This is a real conceptual distinction and it is the reason to keep the entry. An active BCI requires the user to do something deliberate, such as imagining a movement. A passive BCI reads spontaneous brain activity while the user simply behaves normally, and infers workload, stress or surprise. The machine then adapts to the user's cognitive and affective state in real time. The German Cyber Agency describes NAFAS as the first time passive BCIs have been used in this way.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- NAFAS — Neuroadaptivity for Autonomous Systems. The German Cyber Agency awarded Zander Labs a four-year contract of EUR 30M, signed 15 December 2023 at Brandenburg Technical University Cottbus-Senftenberg. The company says it beat four other competitors and calls it the largest single grant for a research project in the European Union.
- Division of work: Fraunhofer IDMT-HSA in Oldenburg develops electrode patches; Zander Labs handles modular system design and optimisation of the passive BCIs.
- Status is unclear. A 2026 Fraunhofer press release says IDMT has successfully completed its work on NAFAS, while the same release gives a November 2027 conclusion date for the project.
- This file recorded the company seeking US partners in 2026. No US partner, collaborator or contract was found, so that remains unverified.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Principally the EUR 30M NAFAS contract. No venture round was identified. A state research contract is a different kind of funding from venture capital: it is more stable for its term and does not imply a commercial product at the end.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Not enumerated, and in Europe. Inferring workload and affective state from spontaneous EEG is a signal-processing and machine-learning problem, so the discipline is right even though the location is not.

ASSESSMENT

Keep this entry for the concept rather than the opportunity. Passive BCI is the idea in this whole group most likely to reach ordinary users, because it asks nothing of them — no training, no deliberate control, no surgery. If brain-adaptive interfaces ever become normal, this is the mechanism. The company is European, state-funded, and its project timeline is reported inconsistently. The claimed US expansion could not be verified. Not a target, but the most useful vocabulary in the group: active versus passive BCI is the distinction to carry into any conversation about this field.
