"Generative Biology" — machine learning that designs novel proteins (antibodies, peptides, enzymes) rather than screening for them. Company operates its own cryo-EM technology group.

- GB-5267 — armoured MUC16-directed CAR-T for platinum-resistant ovarian cancer; Phase 1 planned 2026.
- 5 clinical-stage molecules; 15+ candidates in total.
