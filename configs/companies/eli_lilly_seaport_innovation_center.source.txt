Sector: Established biopharma
Location: Boston Seaport
Summary: $700M site, renamed since launch, and the most acquisitive buyer in the local market.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Lilly announced the Institute for Genetic Medicine in February 2022. It came with a $700M investment in a Boston Seaport site. The building opened in August 2024. The site is now called the Lilly Seaport Innovation Center, shortened to LSC. This file used the launch name until October 2026. Use the new name when you search for jobs, because the old name returns only 2022 press. The building is 15 Necco, a 12-storey life-science building opposite Fort Point Channel, and Lilly leases all of it. The scientific base came in part from Prevail Therapeutics, which Lilly acquired in 2020.

PLATFORM

Genetic medicine. The work is RNA- and DNA-based medicine, which means nucleic-acid therapeutics and gene editing. The site is the central hub for Lilly's genetic medicine programmes. It headquarters that research across Boston and New York. Named disease areas include neuroscience, immunology and diabetes. The site also holds Lilly's first East Coast Lilly Gateway Labs, which rents lab and office space to startups.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Acquired Verve Therapeutics for $10.50 per share plus a $3 contingent value right, up to $1.3B, for one-time in vivo base-editing treatments in cardiovascular disease.
- Also acquired Kelonia and Ajax Therapeutics, both Boston-area.
- Investor in and collaborator with Superluminal Medicines, a deal worth up to $1.3B.
- Prevail Therapeutics, acquired 2020, supplies the gene-therapy base.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Corporate. The building is 12 storeys and 334,000 sq ft. The stated investment is about $700M.

TEAMS AND ROLES

The site holds 500 researchers across biology, chemistry and data science. It also holds 200 employees from companies inside Lilly Gateway Labs. Data science is one of the three named disciplines, so a computational seat is part of the stated plan and not an afterthought. The 2022 announcement gave a smaller growth path, from 120 employees to 250 within five years. The 500 figure is the building's capacity. Do not read the two numbers as one forecast.

ASSESSMENT

Data science is named explicitly in the site's staffing. Lilly is buying Boston assets faster than anyone else, which usually means absorbing their technical teams too. Note the rename before you apply: a search for "Institute for Genetic Medicine" will miss the current postings. The Gateway Labs tenancy is a second, quieter route in, because those startups hire separately on the same floor plate.
