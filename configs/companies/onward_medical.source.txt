Sector: Neurotech - spinal cord stimulation and BCI
Location: Eindhoven, Netherlands, and Lausanne, Switzerland
Summary: Spinal cord stimulation to restore movement after spinal cord injury; brain-spine BCI in trials.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

One source gives 2014 and calls it a spin-off of the EPFL Center for Neuroprosthetics. The company is linked to the EPFL/CHUV work of Gregoire Courtine and Jocelyne Bloch. One source says it was earlier named G-Therapeutics. Registered office: High Tech Campus, Eindhoven; second office in Lausanne.

PLATFORM

ARC Therapy: programmed stimulation of the spinal cord to restore movement and function after spinal cord injury. ARC-EX is external and non-invasive: it stimulates through the skin from electrodes on the back of the neck, with no surgery. ARC-IM is the implanted version. ARC-BCI joins ARC-IM to a brain implant so that thought controls the stimulation.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- ARC-EX: FDA De Novo classification and US market authorization, December 2024 (application April 2024). Company calls it the first non-invasive spinal cord stimulation system for chronic spinal cord injury.
- ARC-EX home use: FDA 510(k) clearance 17 November 2025, for hand strength and sensation in adults with chronic SCI.
- ARC-EX: CE mark under the EU Medical Device Regulation.
- Up-LIFT pivotal study (Nature Medicine): 90% of participants improved strength or function, 87% reported better quality of life, benefit seen up to 34 years after injury.
- Brain-spine interface: Nature, 24 May 2023, 'Walking naturally after spinal cord injury using a brain-spine interface'. Senior authors Bloch and Courtine; EPFL, CHUV, UNIL and CEA-Clinatec.
- The participant, Gert-Jan Oskam, was paralysed in a 2011 cycling accident. After the implant he climbed stairs and walked more than 100 metres.
- The brain implant is WIMAGINE from CEA-Clinatec, Grenoble: it records ECoG from the sensorimotor cortex.
- ARC-IM and ARC-BCI are investigational.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

IPO October 2021 on Euronext Brussels and Euronext Amsterdam, ticker ONWD: EUR 86.5M raised, market capitalisation EUR 391M at listing. Later a Euronext Paris listing. Two European Innovation Council grants.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Not researched. Neuroengineering, clinical and regulatory roles in Eindhoven and Lausanne, plus US commercial roles after the ARC-EX launch.

ASSESSMENT

The only company in this set with an approved device that restores movement after spinal cord injury, and the European academic centre of gravity for brain-spine work. The 2023 walking result is one of the field's landmark demonstrations. For this profile it is a Europe-or-remote option only.
