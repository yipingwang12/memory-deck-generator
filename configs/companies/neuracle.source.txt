Sector: Neurotech - implant BCI (China)
Location: Shanghai, China
Summary: Maker of NEO, the first invasive BCI cleared for commercial sale anywhere.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Legal name Borui Kang Medical Technology (Shanghai) Co., Ltd. (博睿康). SCMP says private, founded 2011. One profile says its NMPA-approved products include digital EEG machines. Some sources place it in Beijing or Changzhou; the NMPA disclosure says Shanghai.

PLATFORM

NEO, developed with Hong Bo's team at Tsinghua University School of Biomedical Engineering. It is semi-invasive: the main device sits in the skull, and eight electrodes rest on the dura over the sensorimotor cortex without entering brain tissue. Wireless. Decoded signals drive a glove that restores hand grasp after spinal cord injury.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Trials began 2023 at Xuanwu Hospital, Beijing. The first participant had complete cervical injury and 14 years of paralysis.
- Plan announced December 2024: 30 to 50 spinal-cord-injury patients at about 10 centres in 2025.
- NMPA commercial approval: 13 March 2026. Reported as the first invasive BCI cleared for commercial use anywhere.
- Per Paradromics: 36 implants (4 feasibility, 32 confirmatory), 18-month follow-up, no device-related serious adverse events (one source).
- First commercial implant reported by mid-2026 (Shanghai).
- One report: national insurance code assigned within 48 hours of approval. Not confirmed.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Filed for a Shanghai STAR Market IPO in June 2026, aiming to be the first BCI company on the A-share market.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Not applicable to this search: China-based.

ASSESSMENT

Not a job target. In the deck for field literacy: China reached commercial approval first, with a low-bandwidth, lower-risk epidural design. Paradromics' rebuttal is fair — approval first is not technical lead — but it marks where the regulatory race stands.
