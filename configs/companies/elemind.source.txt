Sector: Neurotech - closed-loop neuromodulation
Location: Cambridge, MA
Summary: EEG headband that steers brain rhythms with timed sound; sleep first.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2017 (reported 2019 in some sources) by Meredith Perry (CEO), David Wang (CTO; MIT PhD in AI), Ed Boyden (MIT), Nir Grossman (Imperial College London) and Heather Read (University of Connecticut). Came out of stealth February 2024. One of three Boyden co-founded companies in this file (with Cognito and Expansion Technologies).

PLATFORM

Closed loop: EEG sensors read brain oscillations and bone-conduction audio is phase-locked to them, suppressing alpha rhythms to speed sleep onset. Expanding toward focus and cognition.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Direct-to-consumer sleep headband; small studies cut sleep onset by roughly 10-15 minutes.
- The company reports that 76% of subjects in a clinical trial fell asleep more quickly. That is a company figure.
- The headband is the company's first product. Reported prices differ: $349 in one account and $399 in another.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

$12M seed (Feb 2024). Village Global first investor; LDV Partners, MIT's E14 Fund, Wharton Alumni Angels. No later round was found in October 2026, so the seed is still the whole balance sheet after more than two years. Revenue has started, however. The CEO stated on LinkedIn that the company had reached $3.1M of top-line sales since launch, growing 20% month on month in Q4 2025. That figure is self-reported and was not independently checked.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Small team; real-time signal processing and embedded ML. Launch-era coverage put the headcount at 13. That number is from 2024 and is likely stale.

ASSESSMENT

A genuine read-then-write brain device on healthy users, which is rare. But it is seed-stage and has not raised again since 2024. The revenue claim changes the reading a little: a hardware company selling $3.1M of product on a $12M seed is not stalled, it is small. Treat it as a possible later-stage target rather than a current one, and watch for a Series A as the signal to act.

KEY FACTS

- Co-founders include Meredith Perry (CEO) and Ed Boyden
- Closed-loop EEG plus phase-locked acoustic stimulation
- First application: sleep onset
- Suppresses alpha rhythms with phase-locked bone-conduction audio
