Sector: Neurotech - open-source imaging and neuromodulation
Location: San Francisco, CA
Summary: Open-sourced its entire patent and hardware stack; devices now in academic labs.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2016 in San Francisco by Mary Lou Jepsen. Jepsen's background is display and optics engineering at Google, Facebook and One Laptop per Child, which is why the company's approach is instrument-building rather than drug or implant development. In March 2025 Openwater appointed Aaron Timm as CEO; Jepsen remains chair.

PLATFORM

Ultrasound and infrared optics for both diagnosis and neuromodulation, developed as an open platform. The decisive fact is the licensing model: Openwater calls itself an open-source, open-patent medical technology company. Its patents are free to use, its software is released under AGPL 3.0, and its hardware designs are open. An earlier pass dated the open-sourcing to January 2024. This pass did not confirm that date. The model is the opposite of every other business model in this file, where the patent estate is usually the asset — Guardant's cell-free DNA patents being the clearest counter-example.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Open-LIFU — a low-intensity focused ultrasound platform for research neuromodulation, deployed in academic labs as of 2026. A University of Arizona study in Frontiers reported results for Open-LIFU 2.0 in depression.
- Open-Motion — a near-infrared light system that measures blood flow and oxygenation.
- Jepsen presented both devices at a Commonwealth Club event in December 2025.
- Both platforms are for research use only. No regulatory clearance was found.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

In August 2024 Openwater announced $100M in total funding from new and existing investors, including Khosla Ventures, Plum Alley Ventures, BOLD Capital Partners, Esther Dyson and Peter Gabriel. An earlier investor post reported $54M secured. No 2026 round was found.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Not enumerated. An open-source hardware platform has an unusual property for this profile: you can contribute to it, and read its code and designs, without being employed by the company. For someone building a public methods reputation that is a genuine route in.

ASSESSMENT

The most intellectually interesting business model in the neurotech group, and a weak employment target. Open-sourcing the patents means the technology spreads through academic labs rather than through sales, so the company captures little and the field gains a lot. Open-LIFU being deployed in academic labs is the real outcome: it makes ultrasound neuromodulation accessible to researchers who could not otherwise build it. The new CEO from outside the founding team suggests a push towards revenue. Treat this as a platform to use rather than a company to join.

KEY FACTS

- Founded 2016 by Mary Lou Jepsen; CEO Aaron Timm since 2025
- Open-source, open-patent model; software under AGPL 3.0
- Open-LIFU: low-intensity focused ultrasound for research
- Open-Motion: near-infrared blood-flow measurement
- Investors include Khosla Ventures
