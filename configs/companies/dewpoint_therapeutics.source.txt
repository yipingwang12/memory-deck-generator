Summary: Condensate biology, 70% smaller, now a single-asset company in the clinic.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Built on biomolecular condensates: the idea that cells organise biochemistry into membraneless droplets, and that these can be drugged. The science was genuinely novel and the company became the field's flagship. This entry previously read as a stable platform company at 33 words. The real history is harder, and it matters more than the science summary.

PLATFORM

Condensate modulators, which the company calls c-mods. A c-mod changes the physical state of a condensate rather than blocking a single protein site. The approach applies to targets that conventional medicinal chemistry treats as undruggable, including transcription factors.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- DPTX3186 — lead asset and the company's whole focus. A Wnt/beta-catenin condensate modulator for gastric cancer. FDA orphan drug designation in October 2025. First patient dosed in early 2026. Early clinical proof of concept was guided for the end of 2026.
- MYC — preclinical c-mod programme against MYC-driven solid cancers. Research presented at an AACR-NCI-EORTC meeting in October 2025.
- ALS — licensed to Mitsubishi Tanabe for up to $485M in milestones.
- Partnerships continue with Bayer in cardiopulmonary disease and Novo Nordisk in metabolic disease.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

About $287M raised across three rounds. A Series D closed in September 2025 with the amount undisclosed, giving runway into early 2027 and funding the MYC programme. Read the sequence, because it is the story of this entry. Merck and Pfizer broke off partnerships, acknowledged in January 2024. A 15% cut followed that month. In about August 2025 the company cut roughly 70% of its 130 staff, closed its Frankfurt and Dresden sites, consolidated in Boston and narrowed the pipeline, citing its burn rate. The Series D then closed weeks later. So the company survived, but only after two large partners left and two thirds of the staff went.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Small after the 2025 restructuring, and concentrated in Boston. No posting was enumerated. Condensate work is microscopy- and image-analysis-heavy, so the computational content is image analysis rather than genomics.

ASSESSMENT

A cautionary entry, and a useful one. The science is real and still partnered by Bayer and Novo Nordisk, and the lead asset is dosing patients. But two pharma partners walked away and the company shed 70% of its staff before raising again. Runway reaches only into early 2027, and the clinical proof of concept is the event that decides everything. Do not approach before that readout. Keep the entry as the clearest example in this file of a novel platform that did not convert into a durable company.
