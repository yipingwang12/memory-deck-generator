Sector: Genomic medicine
Location: Boston, MA
Summary: Has an approved, growing CRISPR product and a broadening in vivo pipeline.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2013 by Emmanuelle Charpentier, Rodger Novak and Shaun Foy; incorporated 31 October 2013. Charpentier shared the 2020 Nobel Prize in Chemistry with Jennifer Doudna, and the company licensed the foundational CRISPR-Cas9 patent estate for human therapeutic use from her. The Vertex partnership was established in 2015 with $75M upfront and later reworked at around $900M, and produced Casgevy.

PLATFORM

CRISPR-Cas9, spanning ex vivo cell therapy and, increasingly, in vivo editing. The company also has a second editing platform called SyNTase. CTX460 is its first clinical candidate. The company is dual-sited: its releases carry a Zug, Switzerland and Boston dateline, so Boston is a main research site but not the only corporate seat.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- CASGEVY — approved. Q2 2026 revenue $76M, up 78% quarter-on-quarter and 151% year-on-year. FDA approval extended to children as young as 2.
- CTX310 — targets ANGPTL3 for cholesterol and triglyceride metabolism; Phase 1-A showed up to 80% ANGPTL3 knockdown at higher doses. A Phase 1b update is due in H2 2026. Priority indications are severe hypertriglyceridaemia and refractory hypercholesterolaemia. Data went to a late-breaking slot at the ESC Congress in Munich on 28 August 2026.
- CTX340 — refractory hypertension, Phase 1 initiated after FDA IND clearance.
- CTX460 — alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency, Phase 1 initiated. It targets SERPINA1 and is the first candidate from the SyNTase platform.
- CTX321 — a next-generation Lp(a) programme, in IND- and CTA-enabling studies.
- Zugo-cel — lead CAR-T, expanding into autoimmune indications.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Public, NASDAQ CRSP. Cash rising on Casgevy revenue. Liquidity was about $2.36B at 30 June 2026, helped by proceeds from a convertible bond. A tracker put monthly burn near $36M against a $2.44B position from the May 2026 filing. Those two figures come from different dates, so treat the exact number as approximate; the order of magnitude is the point. Q3 2026 results were not out at the time of this pass. The pipeline spans haemoglobinopathies, cardiovascular, autoimmune, oncology, regenerative medicine and rare disease.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Not separately verified this pass. No headcount and no computational requisition was confirmed. The in vivo programmes imply target-selection and genomics analysis work, but that is inference and not an observed posting.

ASSESSMENT

Of the three CRISPR majors, this one has commercial revenue growing at triple digits. The in vivo expansion into common cardiometabolic disease is where the volume is. Two years of runway at the stated burn removes financing risk from the decision. The weak point for this file is that no computational role was ever seen, so the fit rating rests on the science and not on an observed seat.
