Sector: Neurotech - psychiatric neuromodulation
Location: Houston, TX
Summary: Blueberry-sized skull implant for treatment-resistant depression.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Formed through the Rice Biotech Launch Pad. Founded by Rice faculty Jacob Robinson (CEO) and Kaiyuan Yang with physicians Sunil Sheth and Sameer Sheth.

PLATFORM

DOT (Digitally programmable Over-brain Therapeutic): a 9 mm stimulator in the skull, powered wirelessly through magnetoelectric antennas; implanted in a ~20-minute outpatient procedure without exposing or contacting the brain.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Treatment-resistant depression: first-in-human stimulation demonstrated; FDA cleared an Investigational Device Exemption in April 2026 for the first clinical trial.
- The trial is the RESONATE Early Feasibility Study. It may enrol at up to eight US medical centres.
- The primary objective is safety of the DOT implant and the implantation procedure over 12 months of follow-up. Depressive symptoms are secondary measures. Read that carefully: this trial is not designed to prove the therapy works.
- Eligibility: patients who have not responded to at least two courses of medication.
- Motif was selected for an ARPA-H behavioural health programme.
- FDA Breakthrough Device designation for its XCS wirelessly powered implant.
- Partnership with MintNeuro, a London chip company.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

$18.75M Series A (Jan 2024) led by Arboretum Ventures; Max Hodak an individual investor. A further financing was reported as enabling the clinical study to start, but no amount, investor or valuation was disclosed.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Small; clinical, hardware, neural signal work. No posting was enumerated this pass.

ASSESSMENT

A therapeutic BCI for psychiatry, which directly touches Shaohuan's computational-psychiatry target. Houston, and small. Note that Arboretum Ventures is an Ann Arbor firm, so there is a Michigan investor link here. Note also that the entry's strongest claim is regulatory and not clinical: an IDE lets a company start, and RESONATE's own primary endpoint is safety. No efficacy evidence exists yet.
