Summary: AI biosecurity; maps AI-enabled biothreats and pre-builds countermeasures.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

An AI biosecurity public benefit corporation founded in 2025 by Nikolai Eroshenko (co-founder and CEO) and Hannu Rajaniemi. The two spent nearly a decade together as co-founders of HelixNano, a Cambridge biotech, and the founders say Red Queen Bio was spun out of HelixNano. Rajaniemi was HelixNano's CEO, holds a PhD in mathematical physics from the University of Edinburgh, and also writes science fiction, including The Quantum Thief series. The company describes itself as Boston-based. Its purpose is defensive: to anticipate biological threats that AI design tools make easier, and to have medical countermeasures ready in advance.

PLATFORM

Frontier AI models, laboratory automation and reinforcement learning, pointed at threat mapping and countermeasure design. The concrete output is antibody drugs against pathogens. The distinctive part of the thesis is symmetry: the same design tools that could be misused are used to enumerate the threats first and build the defences.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Threat mapping plus pre-built antibody countermeasures. No disclosed clinical programme.
- In January 2026 Red Queen Bio and AbTherx announced a partnership to generate antibodies against targets that Red Queen Bio selects.
- Ian Watson was appointed Chief Government Affairs Officer.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

A $15M seed led by OpenAI, announced in November 2025, with Cerberus Ventures, Fifty Years and Halcyon Futures also participating. One outlet, summarising a Wall Street Journal profile, reports $36M raised in total; that figure is attributed to the WSJ and is otherwise unverified. A single source also reports that Sam Altman and OpenAI board member Nicole Seligman receive shares as part of the deal; treat that as unconfirmed. For context, OpenAI separately backed Valthos, another biosecurity company, which closed a $30M seed with Founders Fund and Lux Capital.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Not enumerated this pass. A company combining frontier models, lab automation and reinforcement learning needs ML engineers alongside protein scientists, but no posting was confirmed and the headcount is unknown. A government-affairs officer suggests public-sector customers.

ASSESSMENT

Interesting and early. The thesis is coherent and the lead investor is strategic rather than financial, which usually means model access as well as money. The location is now confirmed as Boston, which removes the main doubt in the earlier entry. Two things keep it low priority. Almost every number beyond the $15M seed rests on a single source. And biosecurity work is often partly classified or restricted, which limits how much of it can be published or discussed. Do not rely on the $36M figure.
