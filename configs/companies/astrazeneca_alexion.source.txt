HISTORY AND PEOPLE

AstraZeneca opened its new global strategic R&D centre on 5 October 2026. The site is at 290 Binney Street in Kendall Square. It has 570,000 sq ft over 18 storeys. Ten floors hold interconnecting laboratories. BXP, formerly Boston Properties, developed the building. BXP leased the whole building to AstraZeneca before it went into service in Q2 2026. Pascal Soriot, the AstraZeneca CEO, spoke at the opening. Governor Maura Healey also spoke. Earlier reporting said the site would bring about 1,500 R&D, commercial and corporate colleagues together, and that it would also become Alexion's corporate headquarters. Alexion was headquartered at 121 Seaport in Boston, and a spokesperson said those offices would close after the Kendall tower opened. The October 2026 release does not mention Alexion, so treat the Alexion move as reported but not re-confirmed.

PLATFORM

A global strategic R&D centre. The laboratories use robotics, continuous automation and agentic AI. That detail is worth noting: AstraZeneca puts agentic AI in the description of the building itself, not in a separate digital press release. The portfolio covers oncology, cell therapy, chronic disease and rare disease. AstraZeneca is also one of Nabla Bio's generative-protein-design partners.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Broad portfolio; Alexion contributes rare disease and complement biology.
- Named focus areas at the new site: COPD, obesity and metabolic disease, breast cancer, and rare disease.
- Cell therapy and biologics named as growth areas beyond 2030.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Corporate. More than $1B goes into Massachusetts. That sum forms part of a stated $50B AstraZeneca investment in the US. An earlier figure in this file was $3.5B of US manufacturing and R&D through the end of 2026, with over 1,000 US jobs; that came from 2025 reporting. Use the $50B figure for 2026 and keep the older one only as history.

TEAMS AND ROLES

AstraZeneca expects its Massachusetts workforce to grow by more than 50% in the coming years. Nearly 2,000 researchers and scientists will work across 290 Binney Street and the genomic medicine site at 100 Binney Street. The US workforce is above 25,000 and the company claims support for nearly 100,000 US jobs. Specific computational openings were not enumerated this pass. Robotics and agentic AI in the labs imply data and software roles, but no requisition was confirmed.

ASSESSMENT

This is one of the very few large sites that is filling and not subletting. It is now open, not planned, which removes the main risk in the earlier version of this entry. Two numbers also moved up sharply. The workforce figure went from about 1,500 colleagues to nearly 2,000 researchers across two buildings. The US investment figure went from $3.5B to $50B. Look directly at the computational and translational science groups. Kendall Square also gives the easiest commute in 03.
