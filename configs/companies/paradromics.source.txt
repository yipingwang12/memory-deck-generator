Sector: Neurotech - implant BCI
Location: Austin, TX
Summary: High-channel implant for speech; first participant speaking in real time.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2015 by Matt Angle (CEO), Edmund Huber and Andreas Schaefer.

PLATFORM

Connexus: a coin-sized implant with 421 electrodes built for high data rates.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Connect-One early feasibility study (FDA IDE approved Nov 2025); first participant achieved real-time speech and text communication.
- The first Connect-One implant was done at University of Michigan Health. The surgeons were Matthew Willsey, a neurosurgeon and biomedical engineer, and Aditya S. Pandey.
- An earlier first-in-human procedure in June 2025, also with Willsey, showed that Connexus can be implanted, record brain signals and be removed intact in under 20 minutes.
- Target population: restoring independent communication through digital devices for people with spinal-cord injury, stroke or ALS.
- FDA Breakthrough designation.
- NEOM (Saudi Arabia) strategic partnership and planned BCI Center of Excellence within NEOM (Feb 2025). The amount was not disclosed.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

~$88.7M across 13 rounds from 31 investors; Prime Movers Lab lead early investor; NEOM Investment Fund strategic investment. No Series B was found. Note the mismatch: Paradromics has the highest channel count in the implant group but roughly a fifth of Precision's capital and under a twentieth of Neuralink's.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Neural engineering, speech decoding ML, clinical. No posting was enumerated this pass.

ASSESSMENT

Speech decoding increasingly uses large language and speech models, so it is where brain-to-AI integration is most concrete. Austin. One local detail matters more than the location: the first Connect-One implant was performed at University of Michigan Health, and the surgeon-engineer on it, Matthew Willsey, is at U-M. That is a warm-network route you already have standing access to, and it does not require moving to Austin. It is the single most actionable item in the out-of-state neurotech group.

KEY FACTS

- Founded 2015 by Matt Angle and colleagues
- Implant: Connexus, 421 electrodes
- Clinical study: Connect-One
- First Connect-One implant performed at University of Michigan Health
- Connexus was implanted and removed intact in under 20 minutes in a 2025 first-in-human procedure
