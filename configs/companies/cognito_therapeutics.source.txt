Sector: Neurotech - sensory neuromodulation
Location: Cambridge, MA
Summary: 40Hz light-and-sound therapy for Alzheimer's; pivotal readout due 2026.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2016 by MIT's Li-Huei Tsai and Ed Boyden, built on their GENUS (gamma entrainment using sensory stimuli) research showing 40Hz stimulation reduced Alzheimer's pathology in mice.

PLATFORM

Spectris: a headset delivering 40Hz light and sound at home, daily, to entrain gamma oscillations.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- HOPE pivotal study: 670 patients, 70 US sites, 12 months, randomised, double-blind, sham-controlled; primary endpoint MMSE + ADCS-ADL composite; registered as NCT05637801. Patients use the device at home every day for twelve months.
- Enrollment completed July 2025 at the 670 target. Topline guidance moved during 2026: earlier coverage said mid-2026, later coverage said before the end of the year. No topline was found in October 2026.
- Earlier OVERTURE feasibility: 76% less cognitive decline, 77% less functional decline, 69% less brain atrophy over six months versus sham. Important caveat: OVERTURE was not powered to show statistical efficacy, so HOPE is the first real test.
- At AD/PD 2026 the company presented further OVERTURE analysis: treated patients showed less shift in EEG power spectra from higher to lower frequencies than sham over 24 weeks.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

$105M oversubscribed Series C closed March 2026, led by Morningside Ventures, IAG Capital Partners and Starbloom Capital; new investors New Vintage, Apollo Health Ventures and Benvolio Group. Funds the pivotal readout, regulatory submission and a planned 2027 market entry. The round closed before the readout, so the investors are carrying the trial risk and not waiting behind it.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Not enumerated. Clinical data science and EEG analysis are the computational functions. The AD/PD 2026 analysis was an EEG spectral analysis, which confirms that this kind of work is done in-house. Launch-readiness hiring is likely through 2026-27 if HOPE succeeds.

ASSESSMENT

Freshly funded and aiming at a 2027 launch. The HOPE readout is a binary event: a win makes Spectris one of the first commercial neuromodulation therapies for Alzheimer's. Check the result before approaching. Two points sharpen the risk. The guidance slipped within 2026, from mid-year to year-end, and OVERTURE's striking percentages come from a study that was never powered to prove efficacy. So the impressive numbers in this entry are not yet evidence that the device works. Do not approach on the strength of OVERTURE alone.

KEY FACTS

- Founded 2016 by Li-Huei Tsai and Ed Boyden
- Device: Spectris, 40Hz light and sound
- Pivotal trial named HOPE, 670 patients
- Built on GENUS: gamma entrainment using sensory stimuli
- HOPE primary endpoint: MMSE plus ADCS-ADL composite
- OVERTURE was not powered to show efficacy
