Sector: Genomic medicine
Location: Waltham, MA
Summary: Small molecules that drug RNA directly; new CEO in June 2026.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded on the premise that RNA is a druggable target for ordinary small molecules, not only for oligonucleotides. The $38M Series A closed in February 2017, led by Canaan Partners, with Advent Life Sciences, Pfizer, Celgene, Osage University Partners and co-founder Henri Termeer. Michael Gilman was the founding CEO. Mark DeLong was appointed CEO on 25 June 2026. This entry was a three-word `carried` row; it is now `verified`.

PLATFORM

Two named platforms support the same goal. TRYST is the small-molecule discovery engine against RNA targets. PEARL-seq is the sequencing-based method for finding where a small molecule binds a transcript. PEARL-seq is the part worth noting for this profile: it is a sequencing readout, so the platform generates transcript-level data that needs computational analysis.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Myotonic dystrophy type 1 — an oral RNA-targeted small molecule. Data presented December 2025, and the candidate is listed in the company pipeline.
- Stated therapeutic focus: neurology, oncology and rare genetic disorders.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

$38M Series A in February 2017 and a $75M Series B in March 2024. No 2026 round was found. The earlier row in this file implied partnerships with large pharma; Pfizer and Celgene participated in the Series A as investors, but no Amgen or Roche collaboration could be confirmed this pass, so do not assume one.

ASSESSMENT

Scientifically the most interesting unprofiled name in this group, because drugging RNA with oral small molecules would sidestep the delivery problem that just defeated Korro Bio in this same file. PEARL-seq also makes it one of the few entries in this group whose platform produces sequencing data. Two cautions: a CEO change in mid-2026 usually signals a change of direction, and the last financing was the 2024 Series B. Worth a conversation, and Waltham is a car commute.
