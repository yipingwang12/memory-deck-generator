Sector: Neurotech - closed-loop neuromodulation
Location: Mountain View, CA
Summary: The RNS System, a brain implant that detects seizure activity and stimulates to stop it.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

One listing gives 1997 as the founding year. Public on Nasdaq since April 2021.

PLATFORM

The RNS System: an implanted neurostimulator that monitors brain activity continuously and stimulates at the seizure source when it detects abnormal activity. It is closed-loop, unlike most neuromodulation devices, which stimulate on a fixed schedule. Indicated for adults with focal (partial-onset) seizures that two or more drugs did not control. Sources disagree on the approval year (2013 or 2014). Because it records as it treats, NeuroPace holds a large archive of chronic intracranial recordings from people with epilepsy.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Idiopathic generalized epilepsy (IGE): FDA Breakthrough Device designation March 2021; IDE November 2021 for the NAUTILUS pivotal study.
- NAUTILUS: randomized, sham-controlled, up to 100 participants; first implant October 2022. Leads target the centromedian (CM) nucleus of the thalamus.
- NAUTILUS primary endpoint: time to the second generalized tonic-clonic seizure. One-year preliminary results May 2025; 18-month poster (AES 2025) reports significant, sustained reductions in generalized seizure days.
- Next: FDA discussion on an expanded indication. Status not confirmed.
- One source says the DIXI Medical distribution deal ended and became discontinued operations in Q2 2026; one headline reports FDA approval of 'ECoG Assistant'. Neither confirmed.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

IPO priced 21 April 2021 at $17 (top of range), upsized to 6 million shares, Nasdaq NPCE; about $102M gross. J.P. Morgan and Morgan Stanley led. One source says pricing slipped a day after a CFIUS inquiry. Revenue figures are volatile and not carded.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Not researched. Clinical, engineering and data roles in Mountain View.

ASSESSMENT

The longest-running commercial closed-loop brain implant, and in that sense the first therapeutic BCI on the market. Its recording archive is the ML-relevant asset. The IGE expansion is the near-term catalyst. California.
