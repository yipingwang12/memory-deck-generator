Sector: Neurotech - noninvasive EEG consumer and prosthetics

Summary: Chinese BCI company heading for a Hong Kong IPO; the Boston link is historical.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2015 by Bicheng Han, with origins at Harvard Innovation Labs. The location needs correcting. This file recorded Somerville, Massachusetts, with operations largely in China and described the company as nominally Boston. A targeted search found no Somerville connection and reported that BrainCo moved its headquarters to Hangzhou, Zhejiang, in 2018, with an Asia-Pacific headquarters at Hong Kong's Cyberport. Treat this as a Chinese company with a Harvard incubation history, not as a Boston employer.

PLATFORM

Noninvasive brain-computer interfaces, competing with implant companies by avoiding surgery altogether. The product line is consumer and assistive rather than clinical: EEG focus headbands, smart sleep aids, and neural-signal-controlled bionic prosthetic hands.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- EEG focus headbands and smart sleep aids on the consumer side.
- Smart bionic prosthetic hands driven by neural signals on the assistive side.
- Stated use of new funding: accelerating R&D of core BCI technologies, engineering breakthroughs, and scaling production and delivery.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

A private round of about 2 billion yuan, reported between $280M and $288M, led by IDG Capital and Walden International. BrainCo then filed confidentially for a Hong Kong IPO, reported in January 2026, working with China International Capital Corporation and UBS, and potentially raising several hundred million dollars. Because the filing is confidential, those details come from people familiar with the matter rather than a published prospectus.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Engineering is in China. No US or Massachusetts role was identified, and the premise that there is a Somerville operation did not survive checking.

ASSESSMENT

Remove this from any Boston shortlist. It is one of the best-funded companies in the neurotech group and is heading for a public listing, so it is commercially the most successful noninvasive BCI company here — but it is a Chinese company with a Harvard incubation story, and the engineering centre of gravity is Hangzhou. The old row half-knew this, saying operations were largely in China, but still carried a Somerville address and the word "nominally Boston", which overstated the local presence. Confidence rises to `verified` on the funding and IPO facts; the location is corrected.
