Sector: Genomic medicine
Location: Cambridge, MA
Summary: Achieved the first therapeutic RNA editing in humans; now going it alone.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Incorporated in Singapore 23 July 2012, operating from Cambridge. Founded by professors Gregory Verdine and Takeshi Wada, whose founding idea was that the relationship between backbone chemistry and stereochemistry could be engineered rather than accepted.

PLATFORM

PRISM — a discovery platform for stereopure oligonucleotides, targeting genetically defined disease across silencing, splicing and editing modalities. The insight is that oligonucleotide pharmacology can be tuned through three features: sequence, chemistry and stereochemistry. PN chemistry — phosphoryl guanidine-containing backbone linkages, neutrally charged — stabilises the oligonucleotides.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- WVE-006 — alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency. Achieved the first ever therapeutic RNA editing in humans, in the RestorAATion-2 trial. Data from the 600 mg multidose cohort is due in H2 2026.
- WVE-N531 — Duchenne muscular dystrophy, with monthly dosing. Wave guided to a 2026 NDA filing for accelerated approval during 2025. The 2026 language changed: the company is now exploring partnerships ahead of a filing and assessing the regulatory and commercial landscape. Treat the 2026 filing as no longer firm.
- WVE-003 — Huntington's disease. Partnership discussions continue.
- WVE-008 — CTA filing on track for H2 2026.
- Preclinical neurology pipeline.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Public, NASDAQ WVE. GSK returned global rights to WVE-006 after holding them since a 2022 collaboration; Wave states it is better placed to advance the asset quickly for a rare condition. Wave is engaging the FDA on an accelerated pathway. Guidance put that feedback at mid-2026, and a later update placed the meeting at the end of summer 2026. Cash was $544.59M at the Q1 2026 report. Management expects the runway to reach into Q3 2028, and that excludes any GSK milestone payments. This is a large improvement: in Q3 2025 the runway reached only into Q2 2027. An Investor Day was planned for autumn 2026 and may reset the timelines.

ASSESSMENT

A genuine scientific first. The GSK rights reversion is ambiguous — it can be read as confidence or as a partner walking away, and the market took it badly. The balance sheet is no longer the open question: two years of runway into Q3 2028 answers it. The open question moved to WVE-N531. A firm 2026 NDA became a search for a partner within a year, and that is the softer signal in this entry. Read the autumn 2026 Investor Day before you act.
