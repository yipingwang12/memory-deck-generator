Sector: Neurotech - ultrasound neuromodulation
Location: Sunnyvale, CA
Summary: A sub-$500 focused-ultrasound headset for mental health; Reid Hoffman led and joined the board.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

The legal name is Sanmai Technologies PBC, a public benefit corporation. That structure is unusual in this group and signals an intent to prioritise access alongside returns. Jay Sanguinetti is co-founder and CEO. Sanguinetti began exploring brain stimulation as a graduate student. Initial testing began at a Sunnyvale, California clinic for generalized anxiety disorder. Earlier passes recorded a 2020 founding and a Los Altos location. This pass did not confirm either.

PLATFORM

AI-guided low-intensity transcranial focused ultrasound, delivered through a headset. The stated targets are brain regions implicated in anxiety and depression, plus cognitive enhancement; pain, epilepsy and tremor are also named. The strategic choice that distinguishes it is price: a target under $500. Compare Nudge in this file, which uses the same modality inside an MRI scanner over two-to-three-hour sessions. Same physics, opposite product.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- An early clinical prototype targets generalized anxiety disorder. Current trials with the Gen0b prototype run with Acacia Clinics.
- The company is reported to be preparing for FDA clinical trials to validate the technology before wider consumer release.
- Plans to launch in selected research clinics across North America and Europe first.
- No FDA clearance or approval was found.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

A $12M Series A led by Reid Hoffman, the LinkedIn co-founder, reported by Bloomberg in June 2025, with Hoffman joining the board. Totals conflict across trackers: one lists $25.1M of total funding and another $12.0M. Treat $12M as the confirmed round and the larger figure as unverified.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Not enumerated. AI-guided targeting implies imaging and acoustic modelling work, which is physics and signal processing rather than biology.

ASSESSMENT

The most plausible consumer path in ultrasound neuromodulation, precisely because of the price target. A sub-$500 device could reach the scale at which population data becomes available, which is the only route by which this modality would ever generate the kind of dataset a methods person wants. But it is pre-clearance, pre-trial and small, and the funding total is disputed. Reid Hoffman leading and taking a board seat is a real signal, and the public benefit corporation structure is worth noting. California rules it out directly.

KEY FACTS

- A public benefit corporation in Sunnyvale; CEO and co-founder Jay Sanguinetti
- AI-guided low-intensity transcranial focused ultrasound headset
- Target price under $500
- $12M Series A led by Reid Hoffman, who joined the board
- First clinical target: generalized anxiety disorder
