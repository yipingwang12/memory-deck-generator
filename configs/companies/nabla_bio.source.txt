Sector: AI-native TechBio
Location: Cambridge, MA
Summary: De novo antibody design; highest ML purity per headcount on this list.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Launched December 2021 out of George Church's Harvard lab. CEO and co-founder Surge Biswas did the foundational work on the first protein language models, published across Nature Methods and Nature Biotechnology in 2019, 2021 and 2022. Frances Anastassacos co-founded after biotech venture capital and a Harvard PhD. George Church is academic co-founder.

PLATFORM

JAM — Joint Atomic Model — described as "molecular autocomplete": given a disease target, it autocompletes an antibody binding that target at a specified location. Reported double-digit success rates in de novo design across target classes, including picomolar binders to GPCRs in a zero-shot setting, and functional outputs such as GPCR agonists and multispecifics. JAM-2 is the announced model update.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- No proprietary clinical pipeline disclosed; the business is partnership-driven design.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

$11M seed (Khosla Ventures, Zetta Venture Partners) then a $26M Series A led by Radical Ventures — approximately $37M total. Against that, disclosed collaborations exceed $550M with AstraZeneca, Bristol Myers Squibb and Takeda, and a second Takeda collaboration carries success-based payments that may exceed $1B in total.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Small and deal-funded rather than equity-funded. The ratio of partnership headline value to equity raised is the most extreme on this list, which cuts both ways: validation is real, but the balance sheet is thin and the company is exposed to partner priorities.

ASSESSMENT

The purest ML-build seat available. Series A-stage equity risk per 01's table — roughly 10-15% probability of a meaningful exit. Worth noting that its three partners are all cutting their own Boston headcount, which is the outsourcing pattern described in the cross-cutting findings.
