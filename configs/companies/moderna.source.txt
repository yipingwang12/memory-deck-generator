Sector: Established biopharma
Location: Cambridge, MA
Summary: Revenue down 83% from peak; cutting to under 5,000 while promising eight approvals.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

A Flagship Pioneering company, and the most dramatic revenue curve in this file. It is the clearest case of a Boston company that scaled on one product cycle and is now resizing to life after it.

PLATFORM

mRNA, delivered by lipid nanoparticle, applied across infectious disease, oncology, rare disease and latent viruses. The platform argument is that one manufacturing and delivery system serves every programme, so each new target is a sequence change rather than a new process.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Three approved products, with the company guiding to up to eight further approvals within three years across oncology, rare disease and latent viruses.
- Individualised neoantigen therapy in oncology is the programme most relevant to a genomics profile, because it requires per-patient sequencing and variant calling to design the construct.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Public, NASDAQ MRNA. Revenue was $18.4B in 2022, $6.7B in 2023 and $3.1B in 2024 — a decline of about 83% from peak in two years. The company guides to up to 10% revenue growth in 2026 and a $1.5B operating expense reduction by 2027.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Cutting about 10% of the workforce, targeting under 5,000 employees by the end of 2026. No computational posting was enumerated. The individualised-neoantigen programme is the part of the company that structurally requires sequencing analysis at scale, so if a genomics seat exists here, that is where it is.

ASSESSMENT

The fit rating of 1 is right for the company as a whole: mRNA manufacturing and vaccine development do not transfer a single-cell genomics CV. But there is one exception worth naming, and the old 45-word row did not. Individualised neoantigen therapy is per-patient genomics by construction — tumour sequencing, variant calling, epitope prediction — and that is directly adjacent to the current work. If you approach Moderna at all, approach that programme specifically and ignore the rest. Otherwise treat this as a contracting employer through 2027.

KEY FACTS

- Cambridge; a Flagship Pioneering company
- mRNA delivered by lipid nanoparticle
- Targeting under 5,000 employees and a $1.5B opex reduction by 2027
- Individualised neoantigen therapy requires per-patient tumour sequencing
