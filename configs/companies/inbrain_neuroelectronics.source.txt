Sector: Neurotech - graphene neural interfaces
Location: Barcelona, Spain
Summary: Graphene brain implants for recording and stimulation; Parkinson's is the first target.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Spin-off of ICN2 (a BIST centre) and ICREA, and of the EU Graphene Flagship. Co-founders include Carolina Aguilar (CEO; former Medtronic DBS European and global commercialisation director), ICREA professor Jose Garrido, Kostas Kostarelos (later chair of the scientific advisory board) and Anton Guimera of IMB-CNM-CSIC. Founding year is 2019 or 2020.

PLATFORM

Graphene electrodes instead of metal: thin, flexible, and (per the company) able to inject much more charge without the electrochemical reactions that degrade metal contacts. Product concept: an "Intelligent Network Modulation System" that reads and stimulates brain networks.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- First-in-human graphene BCI procedure: during brain tumour resection at Salford Royal Hospital / University of Manchester, UK.
- That study plans 8 to 10 patients and tests safety of graphene in contact with the human brain. Interim results after four patients reported.
- Parkinson's: FDA Breakthrough Device designation in 2023, as adjunctive therapy.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Series A EUR 14.35M (March 2021), co-led by Asabys Partners and Alta Life Sciences. Series B $50M (29 October 2024), led by imec.xpand, with Asabys, Aliath Bioventures and Vsquared; separate funding and support from Merck KGaA to develop the technology in Merck's therapeutic areas.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Not researched. Materials, neural-engineering and clinical roles; Barcelona.

ASSESSMENT

Europe's distinctive bet in invasive BCI: a materials argument (graphene) rather than a channel count argument. The CEO's Medtronic DBS background explains the Parkinson's-first strategy. Early clinical; Barcelona.
