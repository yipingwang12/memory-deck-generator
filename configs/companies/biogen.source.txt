Sector: Established biopharma
Location: Kendall Square, Cambridge (new HQ at Kendall Common from 2028)
Summary: Cutting staff and building a new 580,000 sq ft Kendall headquarters at the same time.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

One of the oldest biotechnology companies in the world and a founding tenant of the Kendall Square cluster. Christopher Viehbacher is CEO. The company reaches its 50th anniversary in 2028, which it has deliberately tied to the opening of its new headquarters.

PLATFORM

Neurology and neuropsychiatry, with rare disease and immunology alongside. Not a platform company: the asset base is conventional biologics and small molecules, so the computational content sits in clinical development, biomarkers and real-world evidence rather than in discovery informatics.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- About $1B of 2025 revenue across LEQEMBI, SKYCLARYS, ZURZUVAE and QALSODY.
- Priority Review for LEQEMBI IQLIK.
- Breakthrough Therapy designation for litifilimab in cutaneous lupus.
- Ten potentially registrational readouts over four years, which is an unusually dense catalogue of binary events.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Public, NASDAQ BIIB. Two property transactions define the current position. Biogen sold 125 Broadway for about $603M in a sale-leaseback while continuing to occupy it into 2028. It then signed a 15-year lease for about 580,000 sq ft of office and R&D space at Kendall Common, a development led by MIT and BioMed Realty, opening in 2028. Reported addresses differ between 75 Broadway and 55 Broadway, so treat the street number as unconfirmed.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Cut about 1,000 positions, to roughly 7,500 worldwide. Those cuts sit inside a cost programme called Fit for Growth, under which the company has reduced its global workforce by roughly 11% since Viehbacher arrived. Against that, the new building is explicitly designed to co-locate R&D and technical operations with the global and North American commercial organisations. No computational posting was enumerated this pass.

ASSESSMENT

Read the two halves together rather than separately, because the headline numbers point in opposite directions. A company shedding 11% of its workforce does not sign a 15-year lease on 580,000 sq ft unless it intends to stay in Cambridge permanently. The cuts are a margin programme; the lease is a statement about location. For a long search that distinction matters: Biogen is not leaving Kendall Square, and from 2028 it will be one of the largest single R&D employers there. Not a near-term target while Fit for Growth runs, but wrong to write off. Ten registrational readouts in four years also means several step-changes in hiring posture, in either direction.

- Signed a 15-year lease for ~580,000 sq ft at Kendall Common, opening 2028
