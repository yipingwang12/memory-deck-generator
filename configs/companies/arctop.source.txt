Sector: Neurotech - software-only brain decoding
Location: Los Angeles, CA
Summary: Software that decodes cognitive state; mostly US Air Force money.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2016 by Dan Furman and Eitan Kay. Furman is CEO. The distinguishing choice is to build no hardware: Arctop sells software that decodes cognitive state from brain signals recorded on other people's devices, with per-user AI models.

PLATFORM

Real-time neural decoding, delivered as software with per-user personalisation. Because it is device-agnostic it is the only pure-software entry in the neurotech group, which also means its value depends entirely on model quality rather than on hardware advantage.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- A $1.25M AFWERX Direct-to-Phase-II SBIR award for cognition-adaptive training simulations. Arctop decodes brain activity in real time during a simulation; BigBear.ai's AI then adjusts the training content in response. MetaTeq and BigBear.ai are subcontractors.
- An earlier US Air Force contract from March 2019 applied the decoding technology to pilot training.
- Federal records show about $3.75M of total contract value across six awards, with the most recent dated 21 March 2025.
- No 2026 award, product or commercial customer was found.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

A $10M Series A announced in October 2023, led by Fifth Growth Fund and Supermoon Capital. This file recorded $16.3M raised in total. Note the balance of funding: roughly $3.75M of federal contract value against a $10M Series A means government work is a large share of revenue for a company this size.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Not enumerated. Per-user model personalisation on streaming neural data is the right kind of problem for a methods person, but the company is small and the most recent federal award is from March 2025.

ASSESSMENT

Technically the cleanest fit of the small neurotech entries, because it is all software and all modelling. The problem is commercial concentration: the visible revenue is Air Force SBIR work, the last award was over eighteen months before this pass, and no commercial customer was found. Defence-funded neurotechnology also tends to limit what can be published or discussed, which matters if building a public methods reputation is part of the plan. Los Angeles rules it out on geography. Low priority.
