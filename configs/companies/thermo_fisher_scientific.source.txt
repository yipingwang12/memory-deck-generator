HISTORY AND PEOPLE

The point this file previously missed: Thermo Fisher is headquartered in Waltham, Massachusetts, and is the largest medical device company in the state. It is not an out-of-state employer with local sites. With over 100,000 employees globally it is the largest single organisation in this dossier, and the only tools-and-reagents giant based here.

PLATFORM

Laboratory instruments, reagents, consumables and services across the whole research supply chain, plus contract manufacturing. Its relevance to a genomics profile is indirect but real: it supplies the sequencing and sample-preparation equipment that the rest of this file runs on, and it operates its own bioinformatics software products. No Massachusetts bioinformatics unit was identified this pass.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Segments span Life Sciences Solutions, Analytical Instruments, Specialty Diagnostics, and Laboratory Products and Biopharma Services.
- The restructuring is concentrated in Laboratory Products and Biopharma Services.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Public, NYSE TMO. As of 31 July 2026 the company had identified restructuring actions expected to produce about $220M of additional charges, mostly in 2026. That estimate came down from about $290M in the previous quarterly filing, which is a mild improvement rather than an escalation.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Read the trajectory, not the total. 2026 severance tied to facility consolidations and cost reduction affected about 1% of the workforce, against about 5% in 2025. So the cutting is decelerating sharply. The Massachusetts specifics: a WARN letter dated 23 January covers closing the Franklin site at 27 Forge Parkway, affecting 103 jobs with separations running from late 2026 into 2027. An earlier notice covered 160 jobs across a Lexington closure and reductions at Plainville and Cambridge, running into November 2026. Note that these figures differ from the ones this file previously held — 300 cut across Cambridge and Plainville, and 80 cut with about 120 relocated at Franklin. The numbers above come from the WARN letters and the quarterly filings, so prefer them, but the discrepancy is unresolved.

ASSESSMENT

Two corrections change how this entry reads. First, it is a Massachusetts company, so it is not going to leave. Second, the cutting rate fell from about 5% of staff in 2025 to about 1% in 2026, and the company said explicitly that the Franklin closure does not signal a broader retreat from US manufacturing. The trend stays shrinking because sites are still closing, but the direction of travel is improving. For this profile it remains an indirect fit: the work is instruments and services rather than biology. Its real use in this file is as a bellwether — when the company that sells the equipment stops cutting, the customers have started buying.

KEY FACTS

- Over 100,000 employees globally
- Closing the Franklin site at 27 Forge Parkway
