Sector: Neurotech - AI EEG for acute care
Location: Sunnyvale, CA
Summary: Rapid-setup EEG headband with AI that detects seizures and delirium at the ICU bedside.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Grew from the Stanford "brain stethoscope": neurologist Josef Parvizi and music professor Chris Chafe turned EEG into sound, and healthy and seizing brains sounded different. In a test, 34 medical students and 30 nurses detected seizures by ear more than 95% of the time. Formerly Brain Stethoscope, Inc.; renamed Ceribell (one source gives August 2015 and founding in 2014). Parvizi is a co-founder and director; one source names Xingjuan Chao as co-founder and CEO.

PLATFORM

A disposable 10-electrode headband and pocket recorder that a nurse can place in minutes, without an EEG technician. The Clarity AI algorithm reads the signal. The current product uses AI, not sound.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- First FDA 510(k) clearance: 2017.
- Clarity: 2023, company calls it the first FDA-cleared AI to diagnose electrographic status epilepticus at the bedside.
- Pediatric use: FDA 510(k), April 2025, patients aged one and older.
- Delirium: FDA 510(k), December 2025, first device for continuous delirium monitoring; same headband.
- More than 450 active accounts and 100,000 patients as of June 2024 (volatile).

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

IPO October 2024 on Nasdaq, ticker CBLL: about 10.6 million shares at $17, the top of the range, about $180M raised. Shares rose about 47% on the first day.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Not researched. ML, clinical and commercial roles; Sunnyvale.

ASSESSMENT

The purest example in this set of AI as the product: hardware is simple, the algorithm is the value, and every new indication (paediatric, delirium) is new software on the same headband. Large-scale EEG ML, close to Beacon's work. California.
