Sector: Adjacent - neuro ML
Location: Boston, MA
Summary: ML on EEG at scale. One of the few genuine two-career addresses.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2019 by Jacob Donoghue, Jarrett Revels, Alexander Chan, Brandon Westover and Sydney Cash. Donoghue holds an MD from Harvard Medical School and a PhD in neuroscience from MIT, where he studied how neuroactive compounds modulate brain network activity. Westover and Cash bring the clinical neurophysiology side.

PLATFORM

A machine-learning platform for interrogating very large EEG datasets, combined with FDA-cleared wearable EEG hardware, to capture and analyse real-world neural data across neurology, psychiatry and sleep medicine.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Precision-medicine platform spanning the full drug-development lifecycle: clinical trial design through biomarker discovery.
- At-home sleep monitoring services for clinical trials.
- Acquired the R&D business of Dreem (Paris, at-home sleep monitoring) in July 2023. Dreem was founded 2014, backed by over $57M, and had recorded over two million nights of sleep.

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

Series B upsized to over $97M in April 2026, bringing cumulative funding above $132M. The upsize added JSL Health, Palo Santo VC, Kicker Ventures and Samsung Next. An earlier tranche was reported at $86M.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Growing on fresh capital. Work is large-dataset ML on neurophysiological time series, which 07 correctly identified as the most comp-bio-like of the neurotech options.
