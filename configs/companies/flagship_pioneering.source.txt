Sector: Venture creation
Location: Cambridge, MA
Summary: Founded five companies in this file. Treat its portfolio as a single job board.

HISTORY AND PEOPLE

Founded 2000 by Noubar Afeyan and Edwin Kania. Afeyan is CEO.

PLATFORM

Institutional venture creation through a staged funnel: Explorations, then ProtoCos, then NewCos, then GrowthCos. Flagship Labs originates 80-100 explorations per year, of which only ten to fifteen become ProtoCos and roughly six to eight become NewCos. Eight to ten ProtoCos are originated annually. A prototyping budget is typically $1M to $1.5M over nine to twelve months, designed to maximise what Afeyan calls the learn-to-burn ratio.

PIPELINE AND PRODUCTS

- Companies in this dossier founded by Flagship: Generate:Biomedicines, Lila Sciences, Cellarity, Tessera Therapeutics, Inari Agriculture.
- Also Moderna, Indigo Ag, Seres, Sana (co-founded).
- 2026 launches: Serif Biomedicines (April 2026, modified-DNA medicines, $50M initial commitment) and Terion (June 2026, digital infrastructure for agriculture).

FUNDING AND PARTNERS

More than 100 scientific ventures created, over $100B in aggregate value, thousands of patents, and more than 50 drugs in clinical development.

TEAMS AND ROLES

Around 55 Computational Biologist openings were listed across the portfolio at the time of this pass. Because Flagship staffs new companies from a central pool, applying into the portfolio is materially different from applying to a single company.

ASSESSMENT

The most important single node in Boston for this profile. The CEO-Partner model means Flagship recruits operators into companies that do not exist yet, which is precisely the warm-network mechanism 01 describes for second and third opportunities.
